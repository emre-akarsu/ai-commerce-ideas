# UK Counsel and Adviser Checklist

As of 2026-10-03. One page per adviser, so a single paid session can close the open points left by the second UK research round (`research/uk2/`, claim IDs in `docs/uk/03-claims-ledger.md`). **None of this is legal, tax or insurance advice; it is a list of questions for people who can give it.** England and Wales unless stated. Scotland and Northern Ireland are not analysed (`profiles/uk-scotland.yaml` and `profiles/uk-ni.yaml` carry unreviewed legal text).

How to use: send the relevant section plus the ledger rows it cites. Each question says what the product currently assumes (profile key or design rule) so the adviser can confirm or change it. Priority: **P1** blocks a paid pilot, **P2** needed before scaling, **P3** monitor.

## E. Founder tasks that need no adviser

- Decide the company-details values for each pilot tenant (name, number, registered office, part of the UK) and load them as tenant identity (currently a deployment setting). Do not onboard a sole-trader or partnership tenant until a variant of the block exists (A1).
- Pay or confirm the ICO data protection fee tier for the operator (the Information Commission is payee from 30 Sep 2026); controllers pay, processors do not.
- Draft the Art 14 privacy notice text and the DPIA; keep the legitimate-interests assessment on file.
- Keep a licence record per reference-data source (edition, date, URL, licence status, proof of permission).
- Re-run the Phase 0 tests that decide whether any of the above is worth paying for (`docs/uk/01-uk-pmf-lean-canvas.md` section 7).
