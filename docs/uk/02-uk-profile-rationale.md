# UK Profile Rationale (`profiles/uk.yaml`, v2)

As of 2026-10-03. For every non-default value: where it comes from, how confident we are, and what must be checked before go-live. **Nothing here is legal or tax advice.** Placeholders are marked. Claim IDs resolve in `docs/uk/03-claims-ledger.md`; the questions for advisers are in `docs/uk/04-counsel-and-adviser-checklist.md`. `profiles/uk-scotland.yaml` and `profiles/uk-ni.yaml` extend this profile and change only bank holidays, the jurisdiction label and one extra notice; their legal text is unreviewed for Scots and Northern Ireland law.

What changed in v2 (second research round): bank holidays are loaded from GOV.UK by nation; an unstated VAT basis is now treated as unknown and sent to a human (the legal position for a VAT-silent price is unsettled, so the profile does not guess); the RFQ asks the supplier to state the basis; company details are required on outbound business email (new `legal.business_identity`); notices and the regulator name were corrected.

## Items the profile cannot express yet (backlog; none is a hard-rule change)

- **Per-supplier data:** the buyer's account number for each supplier (to put in the RFQ), VAT basis, free-delivery threshold, order cut-off, credit terms, quote validity. Needs a vendor field or table (UK-ACC-03 to 05, 08).
- **PO wording:** a clause that buyer terms prevail and vendor terms are rejected, an explicit VAT-basis statement, a clause that substitutes need the buyer's written approval (supplier terms can allow substitution: Hayley 2.5, RS 4.2), goods-in matched to the PO part number, and a supplier-acknowledgement check before goods are accepted (UK-CTL-03, 04, UK-ACC-04). Wording needs counsel.
- **RFQ wording:** "quotation only, not an order". Where suppliers accept email orders (RS, Hayley, Farnell), an RFQ that carries the buyer's account number could be read as an order (inference; UK-ACC-02).
- **Privacy notice link in the footer and the Art 14 text;** a record of the LLM provider's processing region, transfer mechanism and transfer risk assessment (UK-DPL-07 to 09).
- **Construction reverse-charge hint terms** (installation, fitting, labour) and CIS flags (UK-CTL-08).
- **Public-sector rules** (Procurement Act 2023): not modelled; do not sell to public bodies without separate work (UK-CTL-07).
- **Scottish and Northern Irish law differences** and sole-trader and partnership variants (and LLP labels) of the business-identity block (UK-CTL-01).
- **Customs, duty and origin for EU-sourced parts:** a future flag, not implemented.
