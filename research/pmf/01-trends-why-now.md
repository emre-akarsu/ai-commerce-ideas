# Trends and Why-Now for Buyer-Side MRO/Parts Purchasing Agents

## SUPPORTING TRENDS

### 1. Skilled Trades/Maintenance Labor Shortage and Aging Workforce (HIGH RELEVANCE)
Deloitte and The Manufacturing Institute project 3.8M new workers needed 2024–2033 in manufacturing, with 1.9M jobs potentially unfilled, costing the U.S. economy $1T by 2030 (https://reliamag.com/guides/manufacturing-maintenance-skills-gap-statistics/). BLS data shows 159,800 maintenance-technician openings annually, while 75M Baby Boomers retire by 2030 (https://tradecolleges.org/blog/skilled-trades-outlook/). Over 68% of facility operators and technicians are age 45+, with 10,000 Boomers retiring daily (https://www.infodeck.io/resources/blog/maintenance-technician-shortage-workforce-crisis/). **Relevance:** Creates direct demand for automation to offset lost labor; purchases become pressure-driven.

### 2. Maintenance Spend Pressure, Parts Availability, Supply-Chain Volatility & Tariffs 2025–26 (HIGH RELEVANCE)
Global MRO market is $450B (est.) with manufacturing at 31%; spending typically 3–5% of revenue (https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/united-states-maintenance-repair-operations-mro-industry). Airlines paid $3.1B in additional maintenance costs due to supply-chain constraints in 2025 (https://aviationweek.com/mro/supply-chain/early-2026-outlook-shows-mro-market-momentum-increasing). Industrial components represent 44.12% of MRO market share; electrical parts forecast 3.91% CAGR through 2031 (https://throughput.world/blog/spare-parts-management-ai-driven-strategies-for-mro-and-maintenance-excellence/). Tariffs on imported parts (steel, aluminum, electronics from Asia) intensify supply bottlenecks and raise maintenance costs directly (https://www.centerpointgroup.com/blog/5-cost-saving-mro-procurement-solutions-for-manufacturing-facilities/). **Relevance:** Heightens need for rapid spec matching, multi-supplier RFQ, price comparison—core to agent value.

### 3. SMB/Mid-Market AI Adoption in Manufacturing and Facilities (MEDIUM RELEVANCE)
58% of SMBs use generative AI; manufacturing AI adoption is 58% with 48% YoY spend growth in predictive maintenance and quality (https://www.upwork.com/resources/state-of-ai-in-smbs). Predictive maintenance via AI reduces downtime 45% and costs 25% (https://medhacloud.com/blog/ai-adoption-statistics-2026/). 62% of SMB leaders are "very or extremely confident" handing high-stakes tasks to AI agents (est.) (https://www.upwork.com/resources/state-of-ai-in-smbs). Mid-market AI adoption 64% vs. SMB at 42% for 50–499-employee firms; non-information-worker tasks and AIoT remain next frontier (https://techaisle.com/blog/661-top-10-smb-mid-market-predictions-for-2026-and-beyond). **Relevance:** Establishes baseline appetite; CMOs and facilities buyers familiar with AI; less inertia than enterprise.

### 4. Agent Reliability Improvements and Cost-Per-Task Trends (MEDIUM RELEVANCE)
Policy-following (tau-bench retail ~93.5% pass@1 as of Sept 2026; e.g., Opus 4.6) is near commodity (https://pricepertoken.com/leaderboards/benchmark/tau2). Long-horizon reliability decay documented as non-linear degradation over steps (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.29231). Email/document agents widely deployed (Didero 30M Series A Feb 2026, Aron Sep 2026). Quality/hallucination ranks top production blocker at 32% (https://www.secondtalent.com/resources/ai-agents-statistics/). **Relevance:** RFQ-by-email and quote normalization are policy-compliant, short-horizon tasks; off-the-shelf models suffice; no specialized agent costs needed.

### 5. Email/Chat as Default B2B Interface; Supplier Acceptance of AI-Sent RFQs (HIGH RELEVANCE)
Forrester projects 20% of B2B sellers will face agent-led quote negotiations by end-2026 (https://elogic.co/blog/ai-agents-b2b-buying/). Agents handle email RFQs and structured quote intake via EDI/API breaks; 40% of enterprise applications will embed task-oriented agents by end-2026 (https://assistents.ai/blogs/agentic-ai-use-cases-procurement). Didero cites global trade runs on "emails, WeChat, phone calls, POs, packing lists"—email/chat first (https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/12/didero-lands-30m-to-put-manufacturing-procurement-on-agentic-autopilot/). AI agents reduce procurement cycle time by 80% across concurrent projects (https://medium.com/@arunabh223/automating-procurement-with-ai-agents-99e73d78c846). **Relevance:** Email RFQ is table-stakes; supplier inboxes already handled; no new integration needed from buyer side.

### 6. Distributor Consolidation and Distributor-Built AI (MEDIUM RELEVANCE)
Fastenal acquired agentic-AI firm Rampp.ai (Jun 2026) to coordinate sales, inventory, purchasing, supply-chain workflows (https://distributionstrategy.com/2026/09/fastenals-quiet-ai-acquisition-signals-bigger-push-into-agentic-ai/). Grainger acquired Adroit tech assets for $210M (Aug 2026) to strengthen inventory management and layer AI onto customer data (https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/02/04/grainger-ai-sales-marketing-keepstock-tools/). Major distributors moving from buying AI software to acquiring AI companies and talent (https://distributionstrategy.com/2026/09/distributors-move-from-buying-ai-software-to-buying-ai-companies-technology-and-talent/). **Relevance:** Distributors building agent access; validates email/RFQ automation path; buyer agents will interact with distributor agents; creates ecosystem-value play.

### 7. CMMS Market Growth and AI Features (MEDIUM RELEVANCE)
Global CMMS market $1.54B in 2026, projected $2.67B by 2032, 9.6% CAGR (https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/computerized-maintenance-management-system-market-78648926.html). UpKeep secured $50M Series D for AI-driven maintenance automation; Limble launched mobile app (Mar 2026) (https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/01/3373042/0/en/computerized-maintenance-management-system-cmms-market-to-reach-usd-2-67-billion-by-2032-growing-at-9-6-cagr-says-marketsandmarkets.html). Work-order management is top driver; North America 42.4% of global CMMS share in 2025 (est.) (https://technavio.com/report/computerized-maintenance-management-system-market-industry-analysis). **Relevance:** CMMS adoption rising; purchasing agents naturally integrate downstream of work-order creation; positioning opportunity in CMMS workflows.

### 8. Procurement-Software Adoption Among Mid-Market (MEDIUM RELEVANCE)
Procurement software market $8.30B (2025), projected $25.78B by 2035, 12% CAGR (https://www.expertmarketresearch.com/reports/procurement-software-market). Only 60% of large and 30% of small orgs have true Procure-to-Pay; mid-market saw 7% spend-per-employee increase H1 2025 vs. 1% SMB decline (https://www.tropicapp.io/reports/software-spending-trends-2025). Freemium tiers and pay-as-you-grow pricing bring S2P within reach of SMBs; adoption depends on maturity, not size (https://www.spendflo.com/blog/procurement-software-for-small-business-success). Legacy ERP integration complexity lengthens implementation cycles (https://www.procurify.com/blog/2026-mid-market-procurement-benchmark-report/). **Relevance:** Mid-market underserved; low penetration = high greenfield; budget maturity != AI readiness.

## COUNTER-TRENDS (HEADWINDS)

1. **Enterprise incumbent lock-in:** Coupa, Zip, SAP Ariba, JAGGAER expanding agentic features in 2026; enterprise buyers already invested in suites; SMB easier but lower ACV (https://procurementmag.com/news/ai-in-procurement-the-industry-leading-vendors). Relevance: **HIGH** barrier to GTM.

2. **Agentic-AI hype and cancellation risk:** Gartner projects 40%+ of agentic projects canceled by end-2027 (cost, unclear ROI, weak risk controls); 91% of procurement teams stuck in pilots; only 9% at scale (https://www.zycus.com/knowledge-hub/whitepapers/procurement-ai-stalling). Relevance: **HIGH**—buyer skepticism; outcomes > autonomy messaging required.

3. **Supply-chain localization and friend-shoring:** Some sectors (semiconductors, critical minerals) moving to domestic/allied sourcing, reducing tariff pressure and parts-availability variability. Relevance: **MEDIUM**—sector-specific; affects MRO less than capex goods.
