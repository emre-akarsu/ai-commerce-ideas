# UK Competitive Landscape: AI Procurement & MRO Sourcing

Date: 2026-10-02. Research: 12 web searches + teardown review. Method: primary company pages, UK government contracts, Capterra/GetApp. Funding: as reported by cited sources. Not verified: claims without corroborating source named below.

## UK WHITE SPACES

**1. No native UK AI RFQ startup for maintenance buyers.** Procure AI (London) is expanding but enterprise-focused. RFP Quest is tender-discovery-only. No startup ≤3 years old found serving urgent small-value MRO via autonomous supplier outreach.

**2. CMMS tools own the work order but not the supplier quote.** MaintainX, Limble, MRI Evolution all stop at "purchase request"; none integrated supplier RFQ automation or comparative quoting.

**3. MRO marketplace is supplier-owned.** RS (punchout) and Cromwell/Zoro (e-commerce) are distributors with margin; no independent marketplace or comparison engine for multi-vendor sourcing.

**4. Procurement suites are enterprise or public-sector locked.** Proactis ($150M+ revenue) and Basware (1,500 UK public sector orgs) dominate; neither targets sub-50-person maintenance teams or one-off urgent buys.

**5. Subscription model for small maintenance buyers absent.** Aron, Waybill, Procure AI, Fairmarkit all service enterprise or fee-per-transaction; no affordable recurring-fee agent for SMB recurring maintenance.

**6. UK differs from US positioning:** (a) UK procurement culture (OJEU, Crown Commercial, public-sector centralised buying) means compliance-heavy platforms (Basware, Proactis) capture more mindshare; US verticalized agents (Waybill hardware, AAR aviation) less visible. (b) UK MRO market consolidated around two distributors (RS, Cromwell/Grainger); US has fragmented specialist networks (small industrial distributors, fastener houses) that Aron/Waybill exploit. (c) No UK CMMS consolidation play yet equivalent to Autodesk's MaintainX acquisition; UK still greenfield for CMMS-procurement integration. (d) Government AI procurement scheme (£100M Sovereign AI, Aug 2026) incentivizes UK startups (RFP Quest, others) to target tenders, not SMB maintenance.

**Conclusion:** Urgency-driven, one-off maintenance sourcing with autonomous supplier RFQ and approval routing is an open white space in the UK for buyers with 20-200 employees, £50k–500k annual maintenance spend, and no ERP.
