# 05 - UK Voice of Customer: Parts Request → Comparable Quotes → Approved Order

Date: 2026-10-02. Method: WebSearch (12 targeted queries: trade press, FM surveys, vendor reviews, procurement policy, SME adoption research).

## Evidence by theme

### T1. Buying behaviour — channel mix and approval norms

**Hybrid channel model:** UK electrical contractors value both trade counter immediacy and online convenience; price parity between channels required. https://www.electricalreview.co.uk/posts/online-trading-provides-greater-opportunities-for-the-industry

**Phone and field sales entrenched:** 55% of distributors cite field sales as most effective; only 5% rate e-commerce highest. Plumbing/mechanical contractors: most orders by phone; younger 15% digital vs 7% older. https://inddist.com/

**PO approval norms:** UK thresholds typically £1,000 (supervisor), £10,000 (budget), £50,000 (exec), £100,000+ (board). FM sector: £1,000 common threshold. Multi-signature standard. https://www.cleverence.com/articles/for-business/purchase-order-uk-4831/

### T2. Post-Brexit supply chain pain (UK-specific)

**Customs duties spiked 62%:** UK businesses paid £4.8bn in customs duties last year vs £2.9bn prior year—62% increase. Rule-of-origin tariffs on EU-sourced goods add recurring cost and complexity. https://taxjournal.com/articles/62-jump-in-customs-duties-for-uk-businesses-consumers-/

**Lead time impact 2–4 weeks:** Customs clearance adds 2–4 weeks per import cycle. Documentation burden extreme (700+ pages reported). Freight forwarders charge fees; manufacturers charge for paperwork; duties may apply; invoice cycles complex. https://www.bishopfleming.co.uk/insights/avoiding-post-brexit-import-problems

**2025 tariff uncertainty:** 92% of FM operators familiar with latest tariffs; 63% expect significant impact in next 6–12 months, but only 38% have proactively mitigated. Service providers more prepared (70%) than operators. https://fexa.io/blog/why-2025-tariffs-could-upend-facilities-management-plans/

### T3. Inflation, stockouts, and lead times

**Inflation tops pain list (62%):** RS/CIPS 2025: 62% cite inflation as biggest challenge; electrical costs spike on renewable demand. https://theengineer.co.uk/content/news/rs-group-report-highlights-industry-procurement-challenges

**Supply chain disruption (29% rank #1):** CBRE survey: 29% cite as top risk. 30–40% of unplanned maintenance costs from temporary fixes due to shortages. HVAC lead times reach 20 weeks; block manufacturers quoting 4+ months. https://www.cbre.co.uk/insights/articles/fm-procurement-retail-and-multi-site-new-blueprint-for-large-real-estate-portfolios

### T4. Wrong parts, cross-reference, procurement overhead

**Obsolete parts:** SDS Drives and Electric Automation Network offer cross-reference services. Parker/Siemens publish web tools. Outdated part numbers trigger false "no stock" answers. https://www.sdsdrives.com/?p=9891

**Procurement inefficiency:** RS Group: 65% of purchasing resource on 10% of purchases; 50% MRO carriage avoidable; 10% wasted on duplicates. https://uk.rs-online.com/web/content/m/2024-indirect-procurement-report

### T5. Low CMMS adoption in UK SMEs

**Only 20% use CMMS; 44% use spreadsheets:** UK SME survey on computerised maintenance management systems: 20% deployed CMMS, 36% no system at all, 44% rely on spreadsheets. Medium-sized enterprises (51–250 employees) least likely to use CAFM. Implementation barriers present; adoption slower than industrial baseline. https://www.bindt.org/events-and-awards/cm-2023/abstract-2f5/

Implication: Approval workflows, quote management, and parts ordering often live in email and spreadsheets—ripe for agent workflow integration.

### T6. UK AI adoption low for autonomous buying

**54% using AI (2025 BCC):** Rise from 35% in 2024. B2B services 46% vs manufacturers 26%. Only 11% automate operations "to great extent"; 60% usage is content creation. No headcount impact (95% report no change). https://profiletree.com/ai-adoption-rates-in-uk-smes-2025-survey-insights/

**Consumer trust in AI agents: 19% only.** 60% abandon agent after one error; 9% comfortable with autonomous payment. https://itbrief.co.uk/story/uk-shoppers-wary-of-ai-making-purchases-survey-finds

**Implication:** "Quotes + human approval" aligns with low automation adoption (11%) and consumer distrust.

### T7. Vendor reviews — mixed UK experiences

**RS Components TrustScore 4/5 (3,200+ reviews):** Praise for range and online ease offset by complaints of delivery degradation (next-day promised → week-long actual). Price and delivery reliability inconsistent. https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/uk.rs-online.com

**Screwfix TrustScore 2.5/5 (271 reviews):** Lower satisfaction than RS; reliability issues flagged. [Anecdotal from limited sample.]

## Comparison: UK vs US

**Same:** Wrong parts, obsolete parts, approval bottlenecks, stockouts, supplier responsiveness issues.

**Unique UK pains:** Post-Brexit customs/duty (2–4 week lead, 62% duty increase), tariff volatility (63% expect 6–12 month impact), channel hybrid strength (phone + field sales > e-commerce), low CMMS adoption (44% spreadsheets), extreme AI automation hesitation (11% automating operations).

## TOP UK PAINS (ranked by frequency in evidence + UK specificity)

1. **Post-Brexit customs, duty, lead time** (UK-specific; 2–4 week cycles, 62% duty increase, documentation complexity).
2. **Inflation + price volatility** (62% cite; 2025 tariffs add uncertainty; 63% expect 6–12 month impact).
3. **Supply chain disruption / stockouts** (29% rank #1; 30–40% cost impact from temporary fixes; 20-week HVAC lead times).
4. **Lead times extended (4–20+ weeks)** (rail, HVAC, construction blocks).
5. **Wrong parts / procurement inefficiency** (65% resource on 10% of spend; 10% duplicate waste).
6. **Obsolete parts / cross-reference** (dedicated services exist; part-number versioning pain).
7. **Low CMMS adoption** (44% spreadsheets; email-based approval workflows).
8. **Channel friction** (phone vs online; price parity demand; account rep dependency).
9. **Supplier responsiveness** (same as US; knowledge variability at trade counters).
10. **AI autonomy distrust** (54% use AI, but 11% for automation; consumer trust 19%; B2B trust likely similar).

## CONFIDENCE

- **High:** Post-Brexit lead time/duty (tax surveys, shipping reports); inflation (RS/CIPS 62%); supply disruption (CBRE 29%); CMMS adoption (20%); AI adoption (BCC 54%).
- **Medium:** Lead times, wrong parts, cross-reference (trade press, limited quantification).
- **Low:** Channel specifics (Farmington 2023 snapshot; field sales % not measured for FM/MEP).
- **Anecdotal:** Trustpilot reviews small sample; HVAC logic from supplier marketing.
