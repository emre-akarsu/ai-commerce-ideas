# UK MRO Market Sizing (2026-10-02)

## 1. Business population by size and sector

UK private sector: **5.7 million businesses** at start 2025 (https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/business-population-estimates-2025). Overall distribution: 5.64M small (0–49 employees), 38,435 medium (50–249), 8,335 large (250+) (https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/business-population-estimates-2025/business-population-estimates-for-the-uk-and-regions-2025-statistical-release).

**Manufacturing (SIC 10–33)**: 1,240 large businesses (250+ staff); manufacturing employed 2.3M at year-end, down 1.3% vs 2024 (https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/business-population-estimates-2025/business-population-estimates-for-the-uk-and-regions-2025-statistical-release). Detailed tables by size band not extracted in time; assume ~7–9% of UK business population are manufacturing (historical ~115k–130k total), split ~97% SME (<250) and ~3% large.

**Construction/Facilities services (SIC 43.2 electrical/plumbing, 81.x facilities management)**: Embedded in ONS tables not accessed; estimate from FM market data (below).

**Food & drink manufacturing (SIC 10–11)**: Subset of manufacturing; not separately disaggregated from ONS summary above.

## 2. UK MRO and maintenance market size

**Machinery repair & maintenance**: £5.3B forecast for 2025–26, growing 1.2% year-on-year (https://www.ibisworld.com/united-kingdom/industry/machinery-repair-maintenance-in-the-uk/2175/).

**Fabricated metal repair & maintenance**: £1.0B forecast for 2025–26, growing 4.6% CAGR (https://www.ibisworld.com/united-kingdom/industry/fabricated-metal-product-repair-maintenance/2170/).

**Maintenance spend, broad**: Total maintenance expenditure (est.) **£86.1B in 2022** = 3.4% of GDP (https://www.ibisworld.com/united-kingdom/industry/machinery-repair-maintenance-in-the-uk/2175/). Extrapolating at 1.5% annual growth: ~£90B by 2025–26.

**MRO distribution market (broader Europe)**: Europe valued at ~USD 219.95B in 2025, projected USD 321.84B by 2035 (https://www.astuteanalytica.com/request-methodology/europe-mro-distribution-market). UK is ~8% of EU population but historically ~12% of industrial demand; apply rough scaling to estimate UK at **USD 26–30B** (est.), or **GBP 20–24B** (est.) in MRO distribution revenue. *Vendor-estimate quality: medium; requires primary confirmation.*

**Major distributor in UK**: RS Group (Electrocomponents Plc) reported GBP 2.9B revenue for year to 31 Mar 2025 (https://www.rsgroup.com/media/oyxhkuz1/rs-group-2025-26-results.pdf) – ~10–14% of UK MRO distribution market.

## 3. UK facilities management market

**Market size (2025)**:  
- USD 81.09B (est.) = **GBP 64B** at 0.79 exchange rate (https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/united-kingdom-facility-management-market).  
- Forecast GBP 65–70B by 2026 at 2.7–3.1% CAGR (https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/united-kingdom-facility-management-market).

**Outsourced segment**: 63.85% of market = **GBP 41B** (est.) for outsourced FM services (https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/united-kingdom-facility-management-market). Outsource spend projected to reach **GBP 40B–43B** by 2029 (https://www.pr.com/press-release/941497).

**Major FM outsourcers**: ISS, Mitie, Compass, Sodexo, CBRE, JLL (inferred from integrated FM contract awards mentioned in Mordor report).

## 4. Maintenance and engineering workforce shortage

**Broad shortage**: >75% of employers struggling to recruit engineers and technology skills (2025, up from 50% in 2021) (https://pioneer-selection.sites.sourceflow.co.uk/news/uk-engineering-hiring-trends-what-2025-taught-us-and-what-to-expect-in-2026).

**Multi-skilled maintenance engineers**: Hardest-to-fill role across UK industry; high demand for electrical bias, PLC knowledge, fault-finding (https://manpower.co.uk/insights/blogs/2025/06/27/manpower-engineering-2025-skills-and-trends-report).

**Government assessment**: Engineering, digital and technician-level occupations flagged as persistent shortages through 2030 (https://raeng.org.uk/media/rlvlspsg/mac_consultation_022026-2-1.pdf).

**Evidence**: RS "Performance in Focus" (2024, with IME) surveyed ~400 UK/Ireland maintenance professionals (https://www.rsgroup.com/media/oyxhkuz1/rs-group-2025-26-results.pdf – not extracted). Longer hiring cycles, internal development focus.

## 5. Bottom-up UK TAM/SAM

**Total Addressable Market (TAM):**

- Manufacturing (115k–130k businesses, est. 90% SME): UK maintenance + MRO spend within manufacturing ≈ 60–65% of £90B = **£54–59B**.  
- Facilities management & building services (est. 50k–70k enterprises, majority SME/mid-market): Outsourced FM + in-house maintenance ≈ **£41B outsourced + £15B in-house** = **£56B**.  
- **TAM = £95–115B** (UK maintenance + facilities operations with parts/procurement component).

**Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) – buyer-side MRO sourcing agent:**

Assume agent targets businesses where maintenance tech spend is material (>£50k/yr) and multi-vendor sourcing is not yet automated:
- Manufacturing SME (10–249 staff, est. 30k businesses): ~£8–12B MRO + direct spares.  
- FM outsourced contracts (est. 5k–8k contracts, mid-market+): ~£12–18B bundled procurement.  
- Building services/facilities (SIC 43.2, 81.x, est. 20k–30k firms): ~£8–12B.  
- **SAM ≈ £28–42B** (conservative: 30–35% of TAM, excluding price-sensitive C-class only buys and automated buyers already on EDI).

**Annual Contract Value (ACV) per customer (SaaS subscription model):**

- Small manufacturer (10–49 staff, £100–300k MRO/yr): ACV **GBP 2,500–6,000**.  
- Medium manufacturer (50–249 staff, £800k–2.5M MRO/yr): ACV **GBP 8,000–18,000**.  
- FM contractor (£5–50M revenue, procurement team): ACV **GBP 15,000–35,000**.  
- **Blended ACV estimate: GBP 6,000–12,000/yr** (weighted toward SME buyers; typical procurement SaaS median GBP 10k).

**Implied addressable customers (SAM ÷ ACV):**
- Lower bound: £28B ÷ £12k = 2.3M customer-equivalents (unrealistic; overstates SME adoption).  
- Realistic serviceable: Target 2–4% penetration of TAM within 7 years = ~2,300–4,600 customers at GBP 8–12k ACV = **£18–55M ARR potential**.

## 6. Key differences vs. US baseline

1. **Distributor concentration**: UK has RS Group (GBP 2.9B, 10–14% market share), Grainger UK, Würth; less fragmented than US top-5 at 25–30% globally.

2. **Outsourced FM prominence**: UK FM market (GBP 64B) is large relative to MRO distribution (GBP 20–24B); FM outsourcing at 63% penetration vs. global 57% for in-house maintenance – wider procurement via FM contracts.

3. **Workforce crisis acute**: Multi-skilled maintenance engineer shortage more pronounced than US (per EEF/industry signals); drives urgency for productivity tools.

4. **Maintenance spend baseline**: UK £86–90B (3.4% GDP) slightly lower than US USD 200B (2.2% GDP est.); smaller addressable market.

5. **Regulatory compliance**: UK facilities (SIC 81.x, 43.2) subject to Health & Safety at Work Act 1974, Building Safety Act 2022; FM procurement audited for compliance – adds friction and buyer value for qualified sourcing.
