# Agentic Commerce: Consumer / SMB / Merchant Side (as of 2026-10-02)

Method note: figures come from WebSearch result summaries; only the Adobe/TechCrunch page was opened in full (CNBC returned 403). Many secondary blogs (stellagent, digitalapplied, buildmvpfast) are low-grade aggregators; primary sources are flagged where I saw them. Treat "unverified-primary" items accordingly.

## 1. Headline thesis
Agents are a fast-growing DISCOVERY channel with high-intent traffic, but agent-completed CHECKOUT stumbled in 2026: the biggest in-chat checkout (ChatGPT Instant Checkout) was deprioritized and the market pulled back toward "agent recommends, merchant checks out".

## 4. Consumer trust (conflicting by question wording)
- Forrester (Mar 2025 survey): only 24% of US online adults trust agents for routine purchases. https://www.forbes.com/sites/forrester/2026/02/02/agentic-commerces-next-phase-hinges-on-closing-the-consumer-trust-gap/ 
- Gartner: 14% of US consumers trust AI to place orders (aggregator-sourced). https://commercetools.com/blog/agentic-commerce-stats-enterprise-guide
- Accenture (June 2026, n=25,590, 16 countries): 74% would delegate routine tasks; 32% let agent pick product with consumer-authorized payment; 9% fully autonomous incl. payment. https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/ai-shopping-agents-consumer-trust-accenture-report/ (the "74% trust agent more than best friend" line from the same summary is a different framing; treat skeptically)
- Verification habit: 86% of US shoppers who used AI for research cross-checked elsewhere (secondary; https://covergenius.com/resources/blog/do-consumers-trust-ai-shopping-agents/). Croud: 69% open to unapproved purchases (https://www.consumeraffairs.com/news/survey-shows-most-americans-would-trust-ai-to-shop-for-them-081326.html) - outlier, vendor survey.
Take: willingness is high for research/delegating chores, very low (9-24%) for autonomous payment.

## 6. Liability, disputes, fraud
- No jurisdiction has specific agent-purchase liability rules; merchants bear the burden today. https://www.chargeflow.io/blog/ai-agent-chargeback-liability (vendor with interest) ; https://www.worldpay.com/en/insights/articles/agentic-commerce-liability-is-still-being-written
- Mastercard Agentic Tokens and OpenAI/Stripe Shared Payment Tokens encode agent identity; Amex committed to cover erroneous purchases by registered agents. https://thefinancialbrand.com/news/payments-trends/when-ai-agents-make-incorrect-purchases-whos-responsible-197147
- Friendly-fraud window: user authorized agent, then disputes the specific purchase; no framework. https://www.fraudbeat.com/agentic-commerce-disputes-friendly-fraud/
- Behavioral/device fraud signals vanish for agent buyers (Chargeflow, above).

## 7. Bot management vs agent-allowing
- Cloudflare Web Bot Auth / signed agents; "Challenge Agent" rule action; Cloudflare, AWS WAF, Akamai, HUMAN, Vercel verify signatures; DataDome supports it. https://blog.cloudflare.com/signed-agents/ ; https://datadome.co/changelog/web-bot-auth-verifying-user-identity-ensuring-agent-trust/ ; https://nerdleveltech.com/web-bot-auth-ietf-standard-agent-verification (secondary). Web Bot Auth is an IETF draft, not a ratified standard.
- Result: a merchant must now decide per-agent allow/deny/price; tools exist but policy-setting for SMBs is thin (my inference).

## 8. Browser-agent checkout failure modes
- Six production failure modes: DOM drift, screenshot ambiguity, login state, modal interruptions, rate-limit/CAPTCHA, irreversibility. https://futureagi.com/blog/evaluating-browser-use-agents-2026/ (vendor blog; its "78% WebArena vs 22% carts" stat is unsourced - unverified)
- Open CaptchaWorld: humans 93.3%, best model (o3) 40.0% (older, 2025 models). https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.24878
- Real-world confirmation: Walmart cart errors and tax gaps (section 2).

## 9. Loyalty / coupons / price comparison
- Loyalty tiers and member prices are invisible to agents unless exposed as structured data; price-only brands get commoditized; agents surface easy/leaked codes, eroding margin. https://www.dunnhumby.com/resources/blog/loyalty-personalisation/en/agentic-commerce-how-to-win-and-retain-customer-loyalty/ ; UCP supports loyalty credentials and discounts (Google link above). No empirical margin-erosion data found.

## GAPS & OPPORTUNITIES I SEE (ranked)

Merchant-side
1. **Agent-readiness audit + continuous catalog QA for SMBs** (variant/price/stock/tax/shipping correctness, GTINs, UCP/ACP/feed conformance, with "would an agent buy the right item" test carts). Evidence: Walmart wrong items and no tax; Feedonomics is enterprise-priced; GEO tools measure visibility, not transactability. Highest confidence.
2. **Agent-aware dispute/fraud layer**: tag agent-originated orders (signed agent IDs, mandate evidence, intent logs) and auto-assemble chargeback representment. Liability unsettled; merchants hold it; fraud signals degrade.
3. **Agent traffic policy console for SMBs**: one place to allow/rate-limit/price/block agents across Cloudflare/Shopify, verify signed agents, attribute orders per agent. Enterprise bot vendors serve big sites; SMB layer thin.
4. **Machine-readable loyalty/promo layer**: expose member pricing, tiered offers, margin-guarded coupons to agents (UCP supports credentials; merchants don't expose them). Guard against leaked-code abuse.
5. **Multi-surface checkout abstraction**: one integration to ACP, UCP, Copilot, Perplexity, with order/returns reconciliation. Risk: Shopify/Stripe/Feedonomics absorb it; protocols are consolidating.
6. **GEO for SMB/long tail**: Shopify says 75% of AI orders are outside top-100 categories, but Profound/Bluefish target enterprise. Risk: crowded, funded, commoditizing (Scrunch sold, Semrush/Ahrefs entering).

Buyer-side
7. **Delegation-with-guardrails buyer agent for routine reorders** (consumables, subscriptions), where trust data (74% would delegate routine tasks) is strongest; spending mandates, approval thresholds, undo. Avoid "buy anything" positioning.
8. **Cross-merchant price/coupon/loyalty-aware comparison agent** that reads loyalty balances and returns policy; business model via affiliate. Risk: platforms (Google, Amazon) bundle it.
9. **Post-purchase agent** (returns, order tracking, refunds): Salesforce saw agents handle 142% more such tasks in holiday 2025 (https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/2025-holiday-shopping-data/), lower trust barrier than payment.
Avoid: generic autonomous browser buy-agents (platform risk, CAPTCHA/bot walls, trust 9-24%).
