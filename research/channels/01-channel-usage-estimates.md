# Channel usage for B2B RFQ and purchasing: evidence and estimates

Status: desk research on 2026-10-06 using web search only. Most sources are vendor or marketing surveys, mostly US or global, not UK MRO or trades. **The percentages in the estimate table are my judgement, not measured data.** Validate with 10 buyer interviews before relying on them. Product-market fit is unproven.

## Evidence found (as reported by the sources; not independently verified)

| Data point | Source | Caveat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 57% of B2B buyers want quotes delivered by email; 36% want online access to quotes; 34% a phone call | Digital Commerce 360 / Forrester, via search summary | US-weighted, multiple answers allowed |
| Email preferred by 41%, ecommerce 28%, phone 21% (Sapio Research) | [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/338990) | Older (about 2019), sales-contact framing |
| 49% of B2B orders are emailed to customer-service reps; 45% of customers prefer ordering by email; about 70% of B2B orders are processed manually | Conexiom / Workd marketing pages | Vendor marketing, 2025-26 |
| Digital channels (EDI, ecommerce) carry about 48% of distributor B2B sales; the rest is email, phone and similar | Digital Commerce 360 | Distributors, US |
| 61% of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free experience overall, but still want seller input for fit and specification questions | [Gartner, June 2025](https://gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-sales-survey-finds-61-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-buying-experience) | Survey of 632 buyers, about half of activities via reps |
| McKinsey "rule of thirds": buyers want in-person, remote and self-service in equal measure | [Demand Gen Report](https://demandgenreport.com/industry-news/new-mckinsey-research-the-top-three-channels-b2b-buyers-prefer/7426) | Large-deal buying, not MRO spot buys |
| 54% of UK businesses use a messaging app (WhatsApp, Messenger) to talk to customers: 45% of firms under 250 staff, 62% of larger ones | Esendex / PwC report, via [Esendex](https://www.esendex.co.uk/?p=8897466) | Vendor report; mostly B2C customer service |
| One in three UK consumers would prefer WhatsApp or SMS for customer service | [365retail](https://365retail.co.uk/one-in-three-brits-would-prefer-to-reach-customer-service-via-whatsapp/) | Consumers, not buyers |
| WhatsApp is the most used mobile app in construction; formal communication with vendors and subcontractors is still mainly face to face, email and phone | Academic and trade snippets | No UK percentage found |

No source found gave a UK split of channels for MRO or trades RFQs. That gap is itself a finding.

## Estimate table (my judgement, UK mid-size buyers, share of transactions)

| Channel | Send RFQ to suppliers | Receive quote | Approvals inside the buyer | Order or PO to supplier | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Email (text plus PDF) | 65-80% | 65-80% | 25-40% | 45-55% | Medium: several sources agree it dominates |
| Phone and counter | 10-20% | 10-20% | 5-10% | 10-20% | Low: little UK data |
| Supplier portal, e-commerce, punch-out | 3-10% | 5-15% | 0-5% | 15-25% | Low-medium |
| EDI | under 3% | under 3% | 0% | 10-20% | Medium for POs, low for RFQs |
| WhatsApp or SMS | 3-10% (higher for small trades) | 3-10% | 5-15% | 3-8% | Low: no hard data |
| ERP or procurement-suite workflow | 0% | 0% | 25-40% | n/a | Low: depends on buyer size |
| Teams, Slack or chat | 0% | 0% | 10-20% | 0% | Low: hypothesis |

Ranges do not sum to 100% because the estimates are independent and uncertain.

## What this suggests for the product (hypotheses, not findings)

1. Supplier-facing traffic is mostly email and PDF. Keep email as the primary supplier channel and make PDF quotes parse well.
2. WhatsApp is most likely to matter for small-trade suppliers and for the buyer's own approvals, not as the main supplier channel.
3. Approvals inside the buyer are split across email, ERP and chat. Offer an email approval link first, then chat channels as options.
4. Next step: ask 10 UK buyers and 10 suppliers which channel they used for their last three RFQs and quotes, then replace these estimates.
