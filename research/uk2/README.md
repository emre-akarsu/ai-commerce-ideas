# UK research, round 2 (2026-10-03)

Stage 1 (`01-` to `11-`): eleven research reports, each with its own claim ledger (source, access date, quality grade). Stage 2 (`verify/V1a` to `V10`): independent verifiers who did not see the first researcher's reasoning and had to use a different source or recompute from raw data. The consolidated, decision-oriented view is `docs/uk/03-claims-ledger.md`.

How to read them:

- A verifier's verdict is CONFIRMED, PARTLY, CONTRADICTED or UNVERIFIED. The ledger turns these into V, P, X and U; a second agent re-reading the same document counts as P, not V.
- Negative findings ("no such statute", "no such competitor", "no such statistic") cannot be proven; each is scope-limited in the report that makes it.
- Fetch tools summarise pages with a small model, so exact wording must be re-read on the live page before anyone relies on it. Several supplier pages were blocked by their operators; access controls were respected and nothing was scraped around them.
- Search engines mis-date results; the dated facts that matter were taken from the primary page where it could be opened.
- Legal statements are research notes for a solicitor (`docs/uk/04-counsel-and-adviser-checklist.md`), not advice.
