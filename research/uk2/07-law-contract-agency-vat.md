## WHAT ROUND-1 GOT WRONG OR OVERSTATED

1. **"SGA §26" and "SGSA §13" as agency rules.** Wrong. SGA s.26 defines "mercantile agent" for the title rules in ss.24-25 (A3); SGSA s.13 is the care-and-skill term for services (I7). The SGA hook for agency is s.62(2) (A2).
2. **ECA 2000 "recognizes electronic contract formation."** Overstated: s.7 is admissibility in evidence; s.8 is a power to modify legislation (E2-E4).
3. **ETDA 2023 covering "PDFs of RFQs" and "use cryptographic signatures on all PO confirmations."** Wrong. The Act covers listed trade documents; POs, quotations, RFQs and invoices are not listed; it requires no signature (E8-E9).
4. **"UCTA §8 governs battle of the forms; additional terms become part of the contract unless rejected."** Wrong on both counts. s.8 is misrepresentation (I13). Formation is common law and the last accepted terms win (F1-F2). UCTA only tests exclusion clauses in whichever terms prevail (I10-I11).
5. **Apparent authority.** Imprecise: it needs a representation by the principal and reliance (A1). The mandated footer wording was presented as a legal requirement; it is a policy choice.
6. **Procurement thresholds £138,760 and £213,477.** Not supported: PPN 023 gives £135,018 and £207,720 from 1 January 2026 (G1). Round 1 implied private buyers must follow the Act; on s.2 they are outside (G2). The 24 Feb 2025 date is still not directly verified (G3).
7. **Late payment "base + 8%".** Omits the mechanism: 8% over the official dealing rate at the 30 June or 31 December reference date, set by Order (P4, P6-P7); contracting out needs a "substantial contractual remedy" (P5). Its "Interest Order" link (uksi/1998/2765) is unverified; the rate Order is SI 2002/1675.
8. **VAT records "6 years from invoice date … RFQs, quotations, PO confirmations."** Not what Notice 700/21 says: "at least 6 years", no start date on the page, and a list naming orders, invoices and correspondence (V4-V5). "Digital images acceptable" is unverified.
9. **Reverse charge "quotes must state" it.** No source found; the charge turns on specified construction services "reported within the Construction Industry Scheme" (V2).
10. **Omissions.** Round 1 never covered company trading disclosure (§5), and the 2008 Regulations this brief names are revoked (T4).
11. **Not re-checked this round:** round-1 sections on PECR, AI transparency, product safety and insurance.
