## WHAT ROUND-1 GOT WRONG OR OVERSTATED

1. **SGA §26 and SGSA §13 as agency rules:** wrong. s.26 defines "mercantile agent" for title rules in ss.24-25 (A3); SGSA s.13 is care and skill (I7). The SGA hook is s.62(2) (A2).
2. **ECA 2000 "recognizes electronic contract formation":** overstated; s.7 is admissibility, s.8 a power to modify legislation (E2-E4).
3. **ETDA 2023 for RFQ PDFs and "cryptographic signatures on all PO confirmations":** wrong; it covers listed trade documents, not POs, quotes, RFQs or invoices, and needs no signature (E8-E9).
4. **"UCTA §8 governs battle of the forms":** wrong. s.8 is misrepresentation (I13); formation is common law and the last accepted terms win, not "additional terms … unless rejected" (F1-F2). UCTA only tests exclusion clauses in whichever terms prevail (I10-I11).
5. **Apparent authority:** needs a representation by the principal and reliance (A1); the mandated footer wording is a policy choice, not law.
6. **Thresholds £138,760 / £213,477:** unsupported; PPN 023 gives £135,018 / £207,720 from 1 January 2026 (G1). Private buyers are outside s.2 (G2). The 24 Feb 2025 date is still unverified (G3).
7. **Late payment "base + 8%":** omits the 30 June or 31 December reference date, that an Order sets the rate (P4, P6-P7), and the "substantial contractual remedy" test (P5). Its "Interest Order" link (uksi/1998/2765) is unverified; the rate Order is SI 2002/1675.
8. **VAT records "6 years from invoice date … RFQs, quotations":** Notice 700/21 says "at least 6 years", no start date, and names orders, invoices and correspondence (V4-V5).
9. **Reverse charge "quotes must state" it:** no source; it turns on construction services "reported within the Construction Industry Scheme" (V2).
10. **Omissions:** trading disclosure never covered; the 2008 Regulations named in this brief are revoked (T4). PECR, AI transparency, product safety and insurance not re-checked.
