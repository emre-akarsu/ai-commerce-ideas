# Affiliate networks, retail marketplaces and price-comparison APIs as sources of UK product feeds and prices (stage 2 catalogue for a UK refurbishment/MRO purchasing agent)

As at 6 October 2026. Scope: building materials, plumbing, electrical, tiles, fixings.

**Evidence labels.** **[V]** = verified: the primary page or primary PDF was opened and read in this session (for the Awin, Partnerize, Webgains and Tradedoubler PDFs the text was extracted locally and read verbatim). **[U]** = unverified: search-engine snippet, third-party aggregator/vendor page, or a primary page that could not be opened (JavaScript-only portals, 403/404). Quotes are short and always carry the URL.
**Conduct.** No account was registered, no application made and no credential used; everything below comes from public pages only.
**Limits of this pass.** The shared web-search budget ran out part-way, so merchant look-ups for Topps Tiles, CEF, TLC Direct, Plumb Center/Wolseley and Selco rest on a single earlier search and a few direct page fetches; "no evidence found" is not proof of absence. Long legal pages (eBay API licence, Amazon Associates policies) were read through a summarising fetch tool and its clause lettering was inconsistent between two reads, so check clause numbers against the source before relying on them legally. Some terms (CJ, Sovrn, Kelkoo publisher agreement) could not be opened at all.

## Key question 1: Which network or API gives the best coverage of UK trade-materials SKUs, with prices that can be stored and used inside a paid B2B product?

### Takeaway
No network or API in scope is a licensed trade-price catalogue: each is a per-merchant arrangement for promoting merchants through tracked links (or, for Amazon and eBay, a single marketplace). On verifiable evidence Awin has the broadest UK DIY/plumbing/builders' merchant presence with feeds (Wickes, Plumbworld, Travis Perkins; Screwfix UK is closed to new applicants), Impact has B&Q, and Rakuten Advertising reportedly has Toolstation and Jewson (unverified). The specialist trade distributors (CEF, Plumb Center/Wolseley, Selco, TLC Direct, Topps Tiles) showed no affiliate presence in this pass. None of the standard terms read licenses storing prices for a link-less use inside a B2B tool (see Key question 2).

#### 1b. Awin: feeds, fields and how to see which advertisers offer them
- **[V] Two feed formats**: "Enhanced" (Google format) and "Legacy" (standard Awin format), chosen in Toolbox > Create-a-Feed (language, feed list selector, columns, compression, then "Copy the generated URL"). — [Awin: Create-a-Feed](https://help.awin.com/developers/docs/downloading-feeds-using-create-a-feed.md).
- **[V] Scriptable feed list.** Product Feed List Download gives a CSV of "the feeds they can see" with "last update time and date, joined status and download URL", covering advertisers the publisher has joined "or Advertisers that allow Partners to promote their products before joining" (example status "Not Joined"). The download API key comes from a standard feed download and differs from the Publisher API token. — [Awin: Product Feed List Download](https://help.awin.com/developers/docs/product-feed-list-download.md); [Awin: How can I access a Product Feed](https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/How-can-I-access-a-Product-Feed). URL pattern: `https://productdata.awin.com/datafeed/list/apikey/<key>`.
- **[V] The Publisher API programmes list does not flag feed availability.** `GET /publishers/{publisherId}/programmes` accepts relationship values joined, pending, suspended, rejected, notjoined and returns id, name, displayUrl, primaryRegion, primarySector, status, linkStatus etc.; no product-feed field is documented. — [Awin: Get programme information](https://help.awin.com/apidocs/get-program-information.md). So the feed list CSV or Create-a-Feed selector is the way to see who offers feeds.
- **[V] Legacy feed columns** include `product_name`, `search_price`, `store_price`, `rrp_price`, `currency`, `delivery_cost`, `delivery_time`, `in_stock`, `stock_quantity`, `ean`, `isbn`, `upc`, `mpn`, `product_GTIN`, `merchant_category`, `category_name`, `merchant_image_url`, `aw_image_url`, `aw_deep_link`, `last_updated`, `valid_from/valid_to`, `brand_name`, `model_number`. — [Awin: Product Feed List Download](https://help.awin.com/developers/docs/product-feed-list-download.md). Frequency labels (2017 document): `ean`, `delivery_cost`, `in_stock`, `stock_quantity` "Often used"; `product_GTIN`, `upc` "Sometimes used" — [Awin feed column descriptions, Sept 2017](https://cdn.document360.io/ec9ead62-8ecc-46ef-aac7-9b0a9081b40b/Images/Documentation/PM-FeedColumnDescriptions%281%29.pdf). Availability varies: "Column availability depends on the advertiser and their defined feed verticals" and "Empty columns mean the advertiser hasn't provided that information" — [Awin: Product Data Feed FAQ](https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/Product-Data-Feed-FAQ); [Awin: How can I access a Product Feed](https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/How-can-I-access-a-Product-Feed).
- **[V] Enhanced (Google-format) feed via the Publisher Product API**: JSON Lines download at `https://api.awin.com/publishers/{PUBLISHER_ID}/awinfeeds/download/{ADVERTISER_ID}-retail-{LOCALE}.jsonl` (e.g. `en_GB`), OAuth bearer token required. Fields include `title`, `link`, `image_link`, `aw_deep_link`, `availability` (in_stock, out_of_stock, preorder, backorder), `price` and `sale_price` ("15.00 GBP" style), unit pricing, `google_product_category`, `product_type`, `brand`, `gtin`, `mpn`, `condition`, and `delivery.shipping` (country, service, price, handling time). "Currently only retail is accepted"; "Not all Awin Advertisers are currently using the new feed system". — [Awin Publisher Product API (PDF)](https://cdn.document360.io/ec9ead62-8ecc-46ef-aac7-9b0a9081b40b/Images/Documentation/Retail_Publisher_EnhancedFeeds.pdf).
- **[V] Freshness.** "Advertisers often update feeds multiple times a day" — [Awin: Product Data Feed FAQ](https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/Product-Data-Feed-FAQ). Advertiser-side feeds can be scheduled "Daily (up to four times) or weekly"; Awin recommends "an automatic update at least every 24 hours" — [Awin: Managing your feed](https://help.awin.com/developers/docs/managing-your-feed).
- **[V] Images.** `aw_image_url` (200x200) and `aw_thumb_url` (70x70) are served through a caching partner and "Images are cached for 30 days"; the page does not say whether publishers may reuse them independently — [Awin: Resize and Caching Service](https://help.awin.com/developers/docs/awin-resize-and-caching-service.md).

#### 1c. Impact (impact.com)
- **[V] Catalog access for partners**: download in the platform (files under 3 GB), by FTP, or by API (API access must be enabled). Two versions: "Advertiser Format" and "Impact Format" (standardised, 50+ fields covering identifiers, pricing, stock status, media URLs, shipping). The "Last Updated" date reflects changes "which impact.com synchronizes once a day". Files are `.gz`. — [Impact: Download Product Catalogs as a Partner](https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/marketing-content/product-marketplace-and-catalogs/download-product-catalogs-as-a-partner).
- **[V] Product Marketplace** lets a partner search a global catalog by product name, category, manufacturer, brand, GTIN or SKU, including "Other Brands" they are not yet partnered with; tiles show price, description and availability; "Partners can view all products but can only copy tracking links for brands they've joined". — [Impact: Find Products on the Product Marketplace](https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/marketing-content/product-marketplace-and-catalogs/find-products-on-the-product-marketplace).
- Verified UK trade-relevant brand on Impact: B&Q (see 1a). Whether B&Q publishes a catalog to partners is not stated on the pages read.

#### 1e. Rakuten Advertising

Advertiser-side feed spec (id, title ≤70 chars, brand, price, availability, GTIN required; MPN optional; CSV/TSV/XML via SFTP or HTTP) is from vendor pages — [Koongo: Rakuten Advertising](https://www.koongo.com/channels/rakuten-advertising/). #### 1f. Partnerize
- **[U/secondary] Brands publish feeds per campaign**: the Product Feeds API lets brands offer feeds to partners; feeds are "periodically downloaded and parsed for all Partners who participate on the Campaign" so partners' tracking links are inserted; "Any text based file format is supported". — [apis.io: Partnerize Product Feeds API](https://apis.io/apis/partnerize/partnerize-product-feeds-api/). No partner-side download documentation, field list or rate limit was found.
- No verified UK trade merchant from the list on Partnerize.

#### 1g. Tradedoubler
- **[V] Products API for publishers**: needs a Tradedoubler account and a token ("Account" > "Manage tokens", system "PRODUCTS"); "As soon as you are connected to advertisers… you can access their products". Search service returns up to 1,000 products per request with keyword/price/category/brand/availability/identifier filters; an "Unlimited" feed endpoint (`productsUnlimited;fid={feedId}`) returns all raw data for a feed. Fields: name, price, description, image URL, availability, brand, condition, shipping cost, EAN/SKU/UPC/ISBN/MPN, delivery time, etc.; JSON, XML or CSV. When a publisher token is used "the product URL is prefixed with Tradedoubler's tracker URL". — [Tradedoubler Products API (publisher)](https://dev.tradedoubler.com/products/publisher); advertiser-side model (required: name, description, price with currency, image URL, product URL, source product ID, category; optional EAN, MPN, stock count, shipping cost, delivery time) — [Tradedoubler Products API (advertiser)](https://dev.tradedoubler.com/products/advertiser/).
- UK merchants: Victorian Plumbing and Travis Perkins listed as open (aggregator, unverified; see 1a).

#### 1h. Webgains
- **[V] Feeds**: publishers must be "Joined to the program first"; export as CSV, XML or TSV; "the Google Shopping format"; UK mandatory fields id, title, description, link, image_link, availability, price, google_product_category, shipping; optional brand, mpn, gtin, size and up to 10 custom fields; feeds update "up to Daily"; API access to feeds exists. — [Webgains: Product feeds for publishers](https://knowledgehub.webgains.com/home/product-feeds-for-publishers).
- **[U]** Victorian Plumbing's Webgains programme (shown "Closed") reportedly forbids affiliates to "upload the Victorian Plumbing product feed to any external sites" such as Google Shopping or Amazon — [affi.io: Victorian Plumbing](https://affi.io/m/victorian-plumbing). This is the only programme-level feed restriction found for a requested merchant, via an aggregator.

- **[V] Scale/coverage claims**: Kelkoo Group says "10k+ Advertisers" across "29 Markets"; no UK trade-merchant list is given. — [Kelkoo Group: monetise](https://www.kelkoogroup.com/solutions/monetise-your-inventory/). #### 1k. Amazon
- **[V] PA-API 5.0 is replaced by the Creators API.** Amazon's notice says PA-API 5 "has been deprecated"; callers get HTTP 403 "Product Advertising API is deprecated. Please migrate to Creators API." — [Amazon: PA-API 5 deprecation](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/creatorsapi/docs/en-us/paapiv5-deprecation). **[U]** A third-party developer article gives dates: deprecation 30 April 2026, endpoint retirement 15 May 2026; Amazon's pages read give no date — [dev.to article](https://dev.to/th3nate/amazon-pa-api-v5-is-shutting-down-april-30-2026-here-is-what-changes-at-the-auth-layer-22ek).
- **[V] Creators API shape**: REST at `creatorsapi.amazon/catalog/v1/…`, OAuth 2.0 (Credential ID + Secret) instead of AWS Signature V4, tokens valid 3,600 s, camelCase parameters, UK among supported marketplaces — [Amazon: migrating from PA-API](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/creatorsapi/docs/en-us/migrating-to-creatorsapi-from-paapi). Operations: SearchItems, GetItems, GetVariations, GetBrowseNodes — [Amazon: Creators API introduction (amazon.co.uk)](https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/creatorsapi/docs/en-us/introduction). Resources: BrowseNodeInfo, Images, ItemInfo, OffersV2 ("Newer API"), ParentASIN, SearchRefinements, VariationSummary; field-level detail (price, EAN/UPC, MPN) not confirmed in the pages read — [Amazon: resources](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/creatorsapi/docs/en-us/api-reference/resources). GetItems takes "up to 10 ASINs" per request — [Amazon: best practices](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/creatorsapi/docs/en-us/concepts/best-programming-practices).
- **[V] Amazon Business API (UK)**: the Product Search API is "available in… UK, DE, FR, IT, ES, IN"; the "Business Product Catalog" role gives `searchProductsRequest`, `productsRequest`, `searchOffersRequest`, `getProductsByAsins`, `getOffersByOfferIds`; the EU/UK endpoint is `https://eu.business-api.amazon.com`. Specific response fields (price, offers, GTIN) are not enumerated on the overview page. — [Amazon Business: Product Search API overview](https://docs.business.amazon.com/docs/product-search-api-overview); [roles](https://docs.business.amazon.com/docs/amazon-business-roles); [endpoints](https://docs.business.amazon.com/docs/ab-api-endpoints). Some fields are "privileged" (merchantId, ratings, editorial reviews) and `customerReviewsSummary` "can only be displayed in e-Procurement system use cases" — [Amazon Business: privileged fields](https://docs.business.amazon.com/docs/product-search-api-privileged-fields).
- **[V] Punch-out (cXML) is a buyer-side ordering integration**, not a price-data feed: the buyer creates a Punchout group in Amazon Business settings, receives "system login… password (shared secret), Punchout URL and purchase order URL", adds them to the procurement system, and tests with test orders (not charged or shipped); Amazon "supports only new order type. Update and delete are not supported"; order safeguards cover changes to price, delivery lead time and shipping charges. — [Amazon Business: Punchout integration instructions (PDF)](https://static-assets.business.amazon.com/assets/global/documents/guides/Amazon-Business-Punchout-Integration-Instructions.pdf). "Punch-in" is the reverse flow for enterprise e-procurement providers — [Amazon Business: Punch-in](https://docs.business.amazon.com/docs/punch-in-integration-guide).

#### 1m. PriceRunner, PriceSpy, idealo (merchant-side programmes, not data-out APIs)
- **[V] PriceSpy**: merchants log into a Business Center to "add or update your product feed"; product feed free to submit; "Statistics Report" is a paid extra; a separate "Register as a Partner" form exists; the page mentions no API for third parties. — [PriceSpy UK business site](https://business.pricespy.co.uk). PriceSpy says it gets prices "directly from the stores or gather[s] it through automated web scraping" and refreshes "three to five times per day on average" — [PriceSpy: how we compare prices](https://pricespy.co.uk/info/how-we-compare-prices--i5).
- **[U] PriceRunner** (vendor guide): needs "Merchant account and signed agreement with PriceRunner" (UK/SE/DK), XML feed with ID, title, price, shipping cost, stock status, lead time, images, EAN or SKU, brand; feeds "updated daily"; UK prices VAT-inclusive; free listings plus optional CPC — [Koongo: PriceRunner](https://www.koongo.com/channels/pricerunner/).
- **[U] idealo**: the Partner API v2.0 (PWS 2.0) is for merchants to upload offers (needs Client ID, Client Secret, Shop ID) — [Productsup: idealo Partner API](https://help.productsup.com/en/29544-30186-add-idealo-partner-api-v2-0-export.html). An idealo UK affiliate listing on FlexOffers says the programme is "not currently offering" and pays "0.07 GBP for leads and sales" — [FlexOffers: idealo UK](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/idealo-uk-affiliate-program/). Third-party scrapers/monitoring vendors exist for idealo data, which suggests no public data API (inference) — [Dealavo](https://dealavo.com/en/idealo-monitoring/).

### Inferences
- Coverage is the union of merchants that sit on a network and publish feeds. Counting only what was verified, Awin gives Wickes (~12,500 products), Travis Perkins (20,000+ products) and Plumbworld; Impact gives B&Q; Screwfix is closed. Reaching Toolstation/Jewson would mean Rakuten Advertising; Victorian Plumbing would mean Tradedoubler/Webgains; so roughly three or four separate network relationships and per-merchant approvals are needed for about half of the 14 named merchants.
- Specialist trade distributors (CEF, Plumb Center/Wolseley, Selco, TLC Direct) and Topps Tiles did not surface on any network; affiliate feeds therefore under-cover trade-counter supply and over-cover consumer-facing DIY chains and online bathroom retailers.
- Feeds expose public web prices (the Travis Perkins profile notes "logged-in" trade accounts, which a feed would not carry); trade-account or negotiated pricing for a purchasing agent would need a direct merchant arrangement. This is an inference from the profile wording, not a statement in any feed document.
- Amazon Business API is the only programme in scope with an explicit e-procurement orientation and a UK endpoint, and punch-out is a ready ordering channel for Amazon lines; its building-materials SKU depth is untested.

## Key question 3: What are the conditions for a trader or partner to get access (publisher application, merchant approval, API key tiers), including rate limits and cost?

### Takeaway
All affiliate networks require a publisher account approved at the network's discretion, then a separate approval per advertiser programme (merchant approval can be refused or withdrawn at any time); API tokens are free and self-issued once approved. Amazon's Creators API additionally requires an approved Associates account with at least 10 qualifying sales in 30 days and scales its call quota with shipped revenue; the Amazon Business API requires an Amazon Business account, identity verification and Amazon-assigned roles; eBay's Buy APIs are for "approved eBay partners" and need an Application Growth Check to lift default limits. None of the network terms read sets a minimum-traffic threshold at sign-up; ongoing conditions do exist (Amazon's 10 qualifying sales in 30 days, Tradedoubler's suspension after three months without traffic, Kelkoo's start-small traffic and 1% conversion guidance).
