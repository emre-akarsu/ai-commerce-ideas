# UK kitchen refit, domestic electrics (first/second fix, rewire) and full-house refurbishment: public item lists for job-type templates

Scope: UK (mostly England), public sources 2018-2026 where possible. Researched 2026-10-06. Items below are only those that a cited source names; anything not found is in the Gaps sections. Several key primary documents (Contracts Finder kitchen specifications) were found but **could not be retrieved** (HTTP 403 to automated fetches), so the most granular kitchen specs are still missing. Treat this as seed material, not as a validated spec.

Licence note for all council / housing association leaflets below: none of them carried an explicit reuse licence in the retrieved text (checked Canterbury, Cheshire West & Chester, Clackmannanshire, Northumberland). Default position is therefore "copyright of the issuing body, no licence granted"; use for facts/item names (not verbatim reproduction), and confirm before republishing. Gov.uk Approved Documents are Crown copyright; gov.uk content is generally published under the Open Government Licence v3.0, but the OGL wording was not confirmed inside the AD P PDF text that was extracted, so verify per document.

## Kitchen refit: item list, sequence, public specs, and variant questions

### Takeaway
Public social-housing kitchen programme documents give a consistent package order (strip-out, 1st-fix electrics and plumbing, plastering, units/worktops, 2nd-fix plumbing, tiling, 2nd-fix electrics, joinery, decorating, flooring, clean and seal) and a short list of minimum items (3 double sockets above the worktop, a cooker point, washing machine valve/waste/fused spur, humidistat extract fan, 3 courses of tiles, slip-resistant vinyl). Detailed component specs (worktop thickness, carcass construction, sink/tap model) live in Contracts Finder tender attachments that could not be fetched.

### Cited Findings

- Plastering: make good walls and ceiling to an acceptable finish prior to decoration — [CWaC](https://www.cheshirewestandchester.gov.uk/asset-library/council-housing-kitchen-installation.pdf)
- Installation: new kitchen units, worktop and plinths — [CWaC](https://www.cheshirewestandchester.gov.uk/asset-library/council-housing-kitchen-installation.pdf)

- Wall tiling: "three courses above worktop – within the cooker spaces to floor level" — [CWaC](https://www.cheshirewestandchester.gov.uk/asset-library/council-housing-kitchen-installation.pdf)
- Electrics 2nd fix: "Install sockets, switch plates, light fittings, heat alarms, smoke detectors and CO2 [sic, CO] monitors (if required) and test all for safety" — [CWaC](https://www.cheshirewestandchester.gov.uk/asset-library/council-housing-kitchen-installation.pdf)
- Joinery: box in pipework where required, fit architrave and skirting board — [CWaC](https://www.cheshirewestandchester.gov.uk/asset-library/council-housing-kitchen-installation.pdf)
- Painter: decorate with neutral colour — [CWaC](https://www.cheshirewestandchester.gov.uk/asset-library/council-housing-kitchen-installation.pdf)
- Floor coverings: "Installation of a smoothing compound and a damp proof membrane on top of the subfloor. Once dried installation of chosen vinyl floor" — [CWaC](https://www.cheshirewestandchester.gov.uk/asset-library/council-housing-kitchen-installation.pdf)

- Tenant choices: unit colours/styles, wall tiles, floor covering — [CWaC](https://www.cheshirewestandchester.gov.uk/asset-library/council-housing-kitchen-installation.pdf)

**Kitchen items (Clackmannanshire Council kitchen replacement leaflet, Scotland, undated):**
- All existing units incl. sink unit and worktops removed; new base units, wall units and worktops; inset sink fitted into the worktop; worktops extended over washing machine and fridge where possible — [Clackmannanshire leaflet](https://www.clacks.gov.uk/document/2663.pdf)

- New fuse-board fitted if the home has not been recently rewired; new kitchen circuit may involve chasing walls — [Clackmannanshire](https://www.clacks.gov.uk/document/2663.pdf)

### Inferences
- Seed kitchen template packages (from cited sources): Strip-out/disposal; Temporary cooking; Electrics 1st fix; Plumbing 1st fix (incl. radiator reposition); Plaster/make-good; Units + worktops + plinths; Sink/tap 2nd fix; Appliance connections (WM valve + waste + fused spur; cooker point gas and/or electric); Wall tiling (3 courses, cooker area to floor); Electrics 2nd fix (sockets, switches, lights, extract fan, alarms, test + cert); Joinery (boxing, architrave, skirting); Decorating; Floor prep (smoothing compound + DPM) + vinyl; Clean and mastic seal.

## Domestic electrics: first fix vs second fix, full/partial rewire, Part P and BS 7671

### Takeaway
First fix = cables, back boxes, containment before plastering; second fix = accessories, consumer unit connection, testing and the Electrical Installation Certificate. In England, Part P notification is triggered by a new circuit, a consumer unit replacement, or work in a special location (AD P 2013, reg 12(6A)); council rewire programmes publish per-room socket counts that can seed quantities.

### Cited Findings
- First fix covers wiring and containment before plastering: T&E 1.0mm² lighting, 2.5mm² ring/socket circuits, 6.0 or 10.0mm² cooker; back boxes with screws and plugs, grommets, green/yellow sleeving — [MyBuilder](https://www.mybuilder.com/electrical/articles/1st-fix-and-2nd-fix-electrical)
- Second fix: electrician fits accessories (sockets, switches, light fittings, extractor fans), connects the consumer unit, inspects/tests and issues the EIC; the new consumer unit "is normally one of the last things to be fitted" — [MyBuilder](https://www.mybuilder.com/electrical/articles/1st-fix-and-2nd-fix-electrical)
- Material line items with indicative 2026 prices: T&E 2.5mm (100m), 6mm (25m), 10mm (25m); CAT6 (305m); single and double metal back boxes; RCBOs 6-32A; metal CU enclosure (18-way); socket outlets; mains interlinked smoke detectors — [Trade2base, 8 June 2026](https://www.trade2base.com/blog/electrical-first-second-fix-pricing-uk). It also states BS 7671:2018+A2:2022 "requires metal CU enclosures"; note this is an oversimplification (the requirement is a non-combustible enclosure, commonly met by metal) — flag, do not encode as a rule without the BS 7671 text.
- Part P notifiable work (England): "(a) the installation of a new circuit; (b) the replacement of a consumer unit; or (c) any addition or alteration to existing circuits in a special location" — [AD P 2013, para 2.5](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/441872/BR_PDF_AD_P_2013.pdf)
- Special location = within a bath/shower room, space up to 2.25m high (or shower head height) and 0.6m from bath/tray edge (1.2m from shower head centre where no tray); or room with swimming pool/sauna heater — [AD P 2013](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/441872/BR_PDF_AD_P_2013.pdf)
- Non-notifiable: additions/alterations outside special locations, and replacements, repairs, maintenance anywhere; still to be designed, installed, inspected, tested and certificated to BS 7671 (paras 2.7, 3.13) — [AD P 2013](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/441872/BR_PDF_AD_P_2013.pdf)
- AD P 2013 references BS 7671:2008+A1:2011 (the AD has not been reissued, so its BS 7671 reference is dated) — [AD P 2013](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/441872/BR_PDF_AD_P_2013.pdf)
- Routes: registered competent person self-certifies, or registered third-party certifier, or building control; competent person schemes named include NICEIC, NAPIT, SELECT, ELECSA — [AD P 2013](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/441872/BR_PDF_AD_P_2013.pdf); [Trade2base](https://www.trade2base.com/blog/electrical-first-second-fix-pricing-uk)
- Kitchen: adding a socket is not notifiable unless a new circuit is required — [Voltimum Part P guide](https://www.voltimum.co.uk/articles/your-guide-part-p-building)
- City of York Council rewire (council homes, current page): new consumer unit; sockets at 450-1200mm height; minimum sockets: kitchen 3 double + cooker point, living room 4 double, bedrooms 2 double, hall/landing 1 double; hard-wired smoke and CO alarms; RCDs, circuit breakers "and in some cases AFDD"; cables via roof space, under floors and partition walls, surface trunking where concealment not feasible, solid walls cut and replastered; 5 working days standalone (about 10 with other works) plus 3 days for inspection/remedials; decoration pack (paint, filler, roller, brushes, dust sheet, sandpaper) — [York: rewiring council homes](https://www.york.gov.uk/council-homes/rewiring-council-homes)
- Homes for Northumberland rewire booklet (dated 2015, outside window, used for quantities only): living room 5 double, dining 3 double (combined 6), bedrooms 3 double, hall/stairs 1 double per floor, kitchen 3 double + 2 single + cooker point; smoke alarms and outside lights; floorboards lifted on upper floors; existing permitted circuits (showers, security lights) tested and reconnected; 2-3 days; decorating voucher — [Northumberland rewire booklet](https://www.northumberland.gov.uk/NorthumberlandCountyCouncil/media/Housing/Homes%20for%20Northumberland/Electrical-rewire-final.pdf)
- Main bonding to water/gas "may need upgrading to 10mm²" — snippet from council rewire search results ([Ipswich rewire notice](https://www.ipswich.gov.uk/sites/ipswich/files/m-files/rewire_notice.pdf)); not verified in full text.
- Void standard electrical: C1/C2 defects rectified and FIs investigated; electrical safety certificate issued after remedials; at least one light per room; non-fire-rated spotlights replaced; LD2 fire detection; hard-wired smoke alarms and hard-wired CO detector where combustion appliances, per Smoke and CO Alarm (Amendment) Regulations 2022 — [Canterbury Void Lettable Standard](https://www.canterbury.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/Void%20Lettable%20Standard%20policy.pdf)

### Inferences
- Template split: First fix (cables by circuit and size, back boxes metal/plaster-depth, grommets, sleeving, clips/containment, cable routes under floor/loft); Second fix (sockets, switches, fused spurs, cooker control unit, light fittings, extract fans, smoke/heat/CO alarms, consumer unit with RCBOs/AFDD where specified, main bonding upgrade, test and certificate EIC/MWC, Part P notification).
- Variant questions: full rewire vs partial vs new circuit vs like-for-like accessory change (drives notifiability and EIC vs Minor Works); consumer unit replacement yes/no; solid vs stud walls (chasing + replaster vs drop through partition) and surface trunking accepted; AFDD fitted or not; bathroom special-location work; floors accessible (lift boards); kitchen cooker circuit size; alarm category (LD2/LD3, interlinked, CO); per-room socket counts (York vs Northumberland differ — policy choice, make it a profile parameter).

### Gaps
- BS 7671:2018+A2:2022 (and A3 / later amendment status as of 2026) clause text on RCD protection, AFDD recommendation and enclosure requirements was not retrieved; BS 7671 is a paid BSI/IET standard, not reusable.
- No NICEIC/IET or Electrical Safety First public page was fetched in full; Part P points rely on AD P primary text and Voltimum.
- Wales and Scotland notification rules differ (Scotland has no Part P; building warrant regime) — not researched.

## Full-house refurbishment: package order and item lists (void specs, retrofit, PAS 2035)

### Takeaway
Council void lettable standards give an element-by-element checklist (security, clearance, kitchen, bathroom, doors/windows, stairs, loft insulation, electrical, gas, fire, asbestos, water, damp, flooring, decoration, cleaning, external). PAS 2035 frames energy measures (fabric insulation, glazing, ventilation, heating incl. heat pumps, controls) as optional packages that need assessment and design first.

### Cited Findings
- Canterbury void standard elements and items (2026): key safe; rubbish clearance; capital works (kitchen, bathroom, insulation) done in the void if due within 36 months; kitchen/bathroom to Decent Homes; EPC within 5 years; safety checks on heating, electrics, smoke/CO — [Canterbury](https://www.canterbury.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/Void%20Lettable%20Standard%20policy.pdf)
- Bathroom (void): new WC seat; shower screens removed and replaced by curtain pole; new shower head and hose; taps, plugs and chains; three rows of tiles at basin and bath, shower area fully tiled; humidistat extract fan; slip-resistant flooring — [Canterbury](https://www.canterbury.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/Void%20Lettable%20Standard%20policy.pdf)
- Doors/windows: broken/misted glass replaced; every room has a door with working latch/hinges/handle; front and back locks changed with two keys; window restrictors at first floor and above; meter cupboard keys — [Canterbury](https://www.canterbury.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/Void%20Lettable%20Standard%20policy.pdf)
- General: balustrades/handrails secure (BS EN 1991-1-7 / Part K); loft cleared, firebreaks checked; loft insulation minimum 270mm; polystyrene ceiling tiles removed and ceiling made good — [Canterbury](https://www.canterbury.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/Void%20Lettable%20Standard%20policy.pdf)
- Gas: gas fires and tiled surrounds removed, opening blocked, replastered and vented; new LGSR — [Canterbury](https://www.canterbury.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/Void%20Lettable%20Standard%20policy.pdf)
- Asbestos: review register before intrusive work; refurbishment/demolition survey where no information; damaged asbestos floor tiles removed by specialist — [Canterbury](https://www.canterbury.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/Void%20Lettable%20Standard%20policy.pdf)
- Water: dead legs checked; shower heads replaced; damp and mould diagnosed (extract fans/insulation) — [Canterbury](https://www.canterbury.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/Void%20Lettable%20Standard%20policy.pdf)
- Flooring: waterproof slip-resistant coverings in kitchen/bathroom; loose timber flooring fixed; grippers removed — [Canterbury](https://www.canterbury.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/Void%20Lettable%20Standard%20policy.pdf)
- Decoration: newly plastered areas get mist coats of emulsion; damaged wallpaper removed; otherwise tenant decorates — [Canterbury](https://www.canterbury.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/Void%20Lettable%20Standard%20policy.pdf)
- Other published void/lettable standards for comparison (not fetched): [Dorset](https://moderngov.dorsetcouncil.gov.uk/documents/s53202/Appendix%20C%20-%20Lettable%20Standard.pdf), [Torbay](https://www.torbay.gov.uk/democraticservices/documents/s158892/Appendix%208%20Minimum%20Letting%20Standard.pdf), [Tamworth](https://democracy.tamworth.gov.uk/documents/s21820/Appendix%203%20Lettable%20Standard%20for%20Council%20Tenants.pdf), [Thurrock](https://thurrock.moderngov.co.uk/documents/s36960/Appendix%202%20-%20Fit%20to%20Let%20Standard.pdf), [Babergh/Mid Suffolk May 2024](https://babergh.gov.uk/documents/d/asset-library-54706/bmsdc-void-lettable-standard-v-0-2-may-2024-pdf), [Sevenoaks/Quercus Nov 2025](https://www.sevenoaks.gov.uk/download/downloads/id/4701/quercus_housing_void_policy_-_november_2025.pdf)
- PAS 2035 covers assessment of dwellings, identification/evaluation of Energy Efficiency Measures (EEMs), design and specification, installation/commissioning/handover, monitoring; measures include fabric insulation and airtightness, efficient heating/cooling with smart controls, LZC technologies — [BSI PAS 2035:2023 brochure](https://www.bsigroup.com/siteassets/pdf/en/insights-and-media/insights/brochures/pas_2035_2023.pdf)
- Measure examples: roof, cavity wall, floor and external insulation; high-performance glazing; replacing gas boilers with heat pumps; heat recovery; ventilation systems — [FMB: what is retrofit](https://fmb.org.uk/resource/what-is-retrofit-a-quick-guide-to-making-your-home-energy-efficient.html)

### Inferences
- Seed full-house package order (assembled from the kitchen sequence above plus void elements; no single source gives a whole-house order): Survey + asbestos R&D survey -> clearance/strip-out (incl. gas fires, polystyrene tiles, grippers) -> structural/opening works -> 1st fix electrics + plumbing/heating -> plastering/make good -> joinery 2nd fix (doors, architrave, skirting, boxing) -> kitchen and bathroom install -> 2nd fix electrics/plumbing, alarms, test/cert (EIC, LGSR) -> tiling -> decorating (mist coats) -> flooring -> clean -> handover. Optional retrofit packages (loft insulation to >=270mm, wall/floor insulation, glazing, ventilation, heat pump) gated by a PAS 2035-style assessment.
- Variant questions: occupied vs void; asbestos info available; gas present (keep/cap gas fire, gas cooker point); heating change (boiler swap vs heat pump vs radiator reposition only); solid vs stud walls; suspended timber vs solid floors (floor prep, rewire routes); retrofit measures in or out; decoration by contractor vs tenant pack.

### Gaps
- No public line-item spec found for plastering (e.g. board, bonding, skim), flooring materials beyond vinyl, or joinery item lists; nor a heating-change (boiler/radiator) materials list. Void standards specify outcomes, not materials.
- gov.uk Decent Homes guidance (2006) and the Decent Homes Standard review/consultation (2025) component lifetimes were not fetched directly.
- PAS 2035 full text is a paid BSI document; only BSI and FMB summaries were used.
- Contracts Finder full-house/void works specifications (NHF/SoR-based) not retrieved due to 403.
