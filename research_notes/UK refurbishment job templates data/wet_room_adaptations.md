# UK wet room / level-access shower and accessible bathroom adaptations: specifications, item lists and reuse terms

Scope: public UK specifications for Disabled Facilities Grant (DFG) and adaptation work, covering level-access showers and wet rooms, accessible WCs, grab rails and related items. Window 2015-2026. Research run 2026-10-06.

Access problems: several promising sources returned errors to the fetch tools. Contracts Finder attachments and nihe.gov.uk gave HTTP 403. Camden's camdocs server returned 503 and has an incomplete TLS chain. foundations.uk.com gave 403. These sources are listed under Gaps and were not read. Only items I read in full text are listed as extracted.

## Q1. Which councils or housing associations publish DFG/adaptation specifications or schedules of rates listing wet room formers, tanking, anti-slip flooring, thermostatic showers, rails, raised WCs and wash/dry toilets?

### Takeaway
The most complete item-level public specification I could read in full is Walsall Council's "DFG specification for adapted bathroom – Low level Tray" (Version Feb 2015). It is a 17-line priced schedule that names products with "or equal and approved" wording. Council OT advice sheets (North Norfolk, Nottinghamshire) add requirement-level items such as shower area size, WC height and rail placement, but they do not give SKU-level lists. Other candidate schedules exist (Camden DFG schedule of works, Contracts Finder tender attachments, NIHE design toolkit), but I could not retrieve them here.

### Cited Findings

**A. Walsall Council – DFG low-level tray (level-access) bathroom specification, Version Feb 2015** — [Walsall PDF](https://go.walsall.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2022-08/specification_for_dfg_low_level_tray-3.pdf)
The PDF metadata says it was created 17 Feb 2015, and it is hosted in the 2022-08 upload folder. Its stated purpose is "for the use of residents to secure quotations from contractors." Every item below comes from this document:
- Strip-out: isolate, disconnect and remove the existing bath, wash basin (WHB), WC, radiator and other fittings; cart away.
- Door: 138 x 38mm softwood door frame with architrave both sides, 900mm clear opening (if required).
- Walls: hack off plaster, re-board and skim (if required). Shower area to be either 2.5mm [sic] "Knauf Aquapanel" tile board fitted to the manufacturer's instructions, or glazed wall tiles floor to ceiling. Maximum of 2 walls.
- Heating: low surface temperature (LST) radiator, Myson "or equal", with close-coupled TRV kit. Sized for 23°C internal at -3°C external with 3 air changes per hour. Includes drain-down, refill and chemical inhibitor.
- Basin: wall-mounted, Armitage Shanks "Portman 50" with concealed bracket and fixing clamps ref. S1950, lever taps. The hot feed has a **blender valve limiting outlet to 38°C**.
- WC: low-level Armitage Shanks "Sandringham Magnia" or equal. 6-litre cistern with bottom supply and internal overflow, chrome front lever, "Gemini" seat and cover. Includes SVP alterations.
- Shower tray: solid GRP low-level access tray, **35mm maximum height**, AKW Medi-Care "Braddan" range or equal. The contractor corrects floor levels so that no water flows away from the shower area.
- Option 8a, pumped waste where falls are inadequate: Braddan pumped gully, product code 25111, plus "Archimedes 4" waste pump with PGTS.
- Screening: "Carers" shower pack rails and curtains (L or U shape), window spanning kit if needed, 900mm bi-fold doors ("Braddan 2 wet floor screens").
- Grab rails: **2 no. 32mm dia x 600mm fluted grab rails (AKW 1500 series)**, positions agreed with the resident.
- Flooring: "Altro Aquarius" or similar 2.0mm vinyl safety sheet across the whole shower room, with PVC trim. Laid by specialists to **BS 8203**, with welded mitred corners and silicone at sanitaryware to give an impervious floor.
- Ventilation: low-voltage extractor, Vent-Axia "SUX H12" or equal, with through-wall kit. Controlled by a remote humidistat outside the shower enclosure, with pull-cord override.
- Heater: Dimplex down-flow heater (FX20 range) or similar.
- Lighting: sealed ceiling-mounted dust- and splash-proof luminaire (Thorn or Philips or similar).
- Electrics: alter or extend circuits for the light, fan, shower and heater "to current IEE regulations". New 4-way consumer unit if needed. Certificates required.
- Shower: electric thermostatic shower, **Kohler Mira "Advance Flex" 9.8kW** or equal. All exposed pipework chrome. Control height agreed with the resident.
- Tiling: remove old tiles, then fit 152mm square white glazed tiles to 2 shower walls, plus 2 rows above the basin and on the window cill.
- Decoration: mist coat plus 2 coats emulsion (magnolia walls, white ceilings); 2 undercoats plus 1 oil gloss on woodwork.
- Commercial terms: VAT 20% line, with a note that a disabled person's VAT zero-rating declaration may apply. Temporary "porta" WC if the WC cannot be reinstated each day. Occupied-property protection clause.

**B. North Norfolk District Council – Integrated Housing Adaptations Team, "Advice for Self-Funding Disabled Facilities Adaptations: Accessible bathrooms"** (undated in text) — [North Norfolk PDF](https://www.north-norfolk.gov.uk/media/9723/advice-sheet-for-adapting-bathrooms.pdf)
- Names three shower types: (1) level access shower, also called a shaped, dished, sloped or graded floor shower or wet room, with anti-slip floor covering continuous with the floor outside; (2) bath replacement tray, a preformed tray set into the floor with a minimal threshold; (3) low threshold or low-profile tray, used where the floor cannot be breached, e.g. upper floors of flats. A pumped waste can solve lack of fall.
- Shower area: avoid anything smaller than **1000 x 1000mm**; **1100 x 1100mm** is the preferred minimum, measured to the curtain-rail line.
- Shower controls fitted lower, and riser rail extending low, so both can be reached seated. One or two grab rails at the shower entry. Use colour contrast, e.g. dark rails on light walls, and between floor and wall.
- Wall-fixed seat as part of the works, or a free-standing seat from Social Services or NHS.
- Screens are rarely provided; a curtain is preferred.
- Opportunity to fit a slightly higher WC. Rails "on both sides of the toilet" are often requested.

**C. Nottinghamshire County Council – "Level access shower" factsheet** (undated) — [Notts PDF](https://www.nottinghamshire.gov.uk/media/1731835/levelaccessshowerfactsheet.pdf)
- The tray is hidden under anti-slip flooring.
- Shower controls about **1000mm from floor**, usable standing or seated. Pre-set or single-control (flow and temperature) units are suggested.
- Lever taps for limited grip.
- WC centre-line ideally **450mm from the side wall**. Avoid a side-outlet soil pipe so a wheeled commode can go over the pan. Standard pan height of about 16 in (≈406mm) so equipment fits.
- Anti-slip flooring in all wet areas. Choice of wall-mounted drop-down shower seat versus free-standing chair or stool.
- Grab rails in the shower area. Full-length curtain preferred over screens. Contrasting colours, e.g. a dark blue rail on white.
- Wider doorway, or a sliding or outward-opening door, to reduce entrapment after a fall.

### Inferences
- Walsall is a good template for a "low-level tray / level-access shower conversion" bill of materials. Its line structure (strip-out, boarding/tanking, LST radiator, basin + TMV/blender, WC, tray/former + waste/pump, curtain/screens, grab rails, safety vinyl, extractor + humidistat, downflow heater, IP luminaire, electrics, electric thermostatic shower, tiling, decoration) maps directly onto a job template. It is 2015-dated, so the named models (Mira Advance Flex 9.8kW, AKW Braddan, Vent-Axia SUX H12) should be treated as "or equal" placeholders and checked for current availability.
- Walsall does not mention a sealed wet-room former (e.g. a timber-floor former), a separate liquid tanking membrane, drop-down/hinged support rails at the WC, or a wash/dry toilet. These items will need another source.

### Gaps
- No full-text item list was obtained for: wet-room formers on timber or concrete; liquid or sheet tanking kits; drop-down WC support rails; wash/dry toilets (Closomat/Geberit AquaClean); raised WC pans or plinths. The sources that might list them (Camden, Contracts Finder, NIHE, Foundations) were blocked.
- No openly published council or HIA schedule of rates with prices was retrieved.
- Foundations (foundations.uk.com) returned 403, so I could not confirm whether it publishes a model specification.

## Q2. Which standards apply, and what item-level requirements follow?

### Takeaway
Approved Document M Vol 1 (2015 incl. 2016 amendments, OGL) is the only standard here with readable, citable item-level text. It defines installed and potential level-access showers and says the wet room needs a floor gully. It sets wall reinforcement for rails at 1.5 kN/m², and for M4(3) a WC pan of at least 400mm, a front lever flush on the transfer side, a wall-hung basin with rim at 770-850mm, and a ceiling able to take a 200 kg hoist. Walsall cites BS 8203 for sheet vinyl installation. BS 8300 and BS 6465 are paywalled and were not consulted, so their figures are not reported here.

### Cited Findings
From [Approved Document M Vol 1, 2015 incl. 2016 amendments (gov.uk PDF)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7f8a82ed915d74e622b17b/BR_PDF_AD_M1_2015_with_2016_amendments_V3.pdf):
- "Installed level access shower": a "step-free area with no lips or upstands, suitable for showering, with a floor laid to shallow falls towards a floor gulley connected to the drainage system" (Key terms).
- "Potential level access shower": space for a level-access shower "without the need to move walls, remove screed or other solid flooring". It "should include a capped-off floor gulley, set at an appropriate level and connected to the drainage system (Usually provided within a wet room)." **Item: floor gully with capping.**
- M4(2) para 2.26: walls, ducts and boxings to WC, bathroom and shower room must support grab rails, seats and adaptations of up to **1.5 kN/m²**. A note says high point loads may need extra localised strengthening. **Item: noggins, pattress or plywood backing.**
- M4(2) para 2.27c / 2.29c: the entrance-storey WC (in 3+ bedroom homes) and the main bathroom provide an installed or potential level-access shower.
- M4(3) para 3.36: (c) every room with an installed level-access shower is built as a **wet room**; (d) walls take 1.5 kN/m²; (e) the ceiling takes an **overhead hoist of 200 kg**; (f) WC flush is a **lever on the front of the cistern, on the transfer side**; (g) **WC pan at least 400mm high**; (h) basins **wall-hung, rim 770-850mm** above floor, with a clear zone beneath ideally 400-600mm; (i) SVP only beside the WC if unavoidable, and on the wall side.
- M4(3) diagram notes give the shower space as **1200 x 1200mm (1000 x 1000mm permitted in some cases)**. This is from the extracted diagram text; the exact condition was not fully legible.
- Glossary gradients: "Level" means a gradient not exceeding 1:60; "gently sloping" means 1:60-1:20. ADM Vol 1 states **no specific shower floor fall**. No "1:40" or other shower gradient appears in the text.
- Further FAQ — [MHCLG FAQ ADM Vol1 & Vol2](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/648232/FAQ_ADM_Vol1_and_Vol2.pdf) (not read in detail).

Other requirement-level items:
- Sheet vinyl safety flooring installed to **BS 8203**, with welded seams and sealed to sanitaryware — [Walsall](https://go.walsall.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2022-08/specification_for_dfg_low_level_tray-3.pdf).
- Hot water to the basin limited to **38°C** by a blender valve (Walsall's own requirement, not a standard citation) — [Walsall](https://go.walsall.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2022-08/specification_for_dfg_low_level_tray-3.pdf).
- Electrical work to "current IEE regulations" (BS 7671) with certification — [Walsall](https://go.walsall.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2022-08/specification_for_dfg_low_level_tray-3.pdf).
- Product listings label TMV3-approved shower valves as conforming to BS EN 1111 / BS EN 1287. This is a retailer source, not a standard — [Independent4Life Triton Elina TMV3 kit](https://independent4life.co.uk/triton-elina-exposed-concentric-tmv3-mixer-shower-with-grab-riser-kit).
- Scotland: the Domestic Technical Handbook (standard 3.12) allows a level-access floor shower with a drained area of at least 1.0 x 1.0m, or a 900 x 900mm tray — [gov.scot Technical Handbook 2022 domestic 3.12](https://www.gov.scot/publications/building-standards-technical-handbook-2022-domestic/3-environment/3-12-sanitary-facilities/). Taken from the search summary and not verified on the page.

### Inferences
- A template for "wet room conversion" can justify these ADM items: floor gully with drainage connection, a wet-room floor (former plus sealed safety vinyl or tanked tiling), wall reinforcement for rails and seats, a ≥400mm WC pan, a front-lever cistern on the transfer side, a wall-hung basin with lever taps, and optional hoist-rated ceiling works.
- A specific drain gradient (often quoted in trade literature as about 1:40 to 1:80) is **not** stated in ADM Vol 1. It should come from the former or gully manufacturer's instructions, or from a paywalled standard (BS EN 1253 gullies / BS 6465), not from ADM.

### Gaps
- BS 8300-2:2018 values (e.g. grab rail diameter 32-35mm, drop-down rail heights, WC set-out, TMV and shower outlet temperature) were not consulted because the standard is paywalled. Do not quote them without checking.
- BS 6465-1/-2/-3 (sanitary provision and installation) and BS EN 1253 (gullies) were not consulted.
- No public OT body guidance with item lists (e.g. RCOT, or the Foundations/HIA "Adaptations without delay" guide) was retrieved.

## Q3. Which documents are OGL or otherwise reusable?

### Takeaway
Only the Approved Document M PDF carries an explicit Open Government Licence v3 statement. Council DFG specifications and factsheets carry no licence text, so reuse status is uncertain. Treat them as citable for facts and structure, but do not republish them verbatim without asking the council. The M3NHF Schedule of Rates (the de facto housing-association SOR) is "All rights reserved" and must not be reused.

### Cited Findings
- Approved Document M Vol 1: "© Crown copyright, 2015. You may re-use this information (excluding logos) free of charge in any format or medium, under the terms of the Open Government Licence v3." — [ADM Vol 1 PDF](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7f8a82ed915d74e622b17b/BR_PDF_AD_M1_2015_with_2016_amendments_V3.pdf). **Licence: OGL v3, confirmed.**
- Walsall DFG low-level tray spec: no copyright or licence statement in the 4-page text. It says the document is "for the use of residents to secure quotations" — [Walsall](https://go.walsall.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2022-08/specification_for_dfg_low_level_tray-3.pdf). **Licence: unknown.** Local authority material is not automatically OGL. Some councils adopt OGL for website content, but I did not check Walsall's terms.
- North Norfolk advice sheet — [PDF](https://www.north-norfolk.gov.uk/media/9723/advice-sheet-for-adapting-bathrooms.pdf), and Nottinghamshire factsheet — [PDF](https://www.nottinghamshire.gov.uk/media/1731835/levelaccessshowerfactsheet.pdf): no licence text in the extracted content. **Licence: unknown.**
- gov.scot Technical Handbook pages are typically published under OGL, but I did not verify this on the page — [gov.scot](https://www.gov.scot/publications/building-standards-technical-handbook-2022-domestic/3-environment/3-12-sanitary-facilities/). **Licence: likely OGL, unverified.**
- Contracts Finder and Find a Tender: the notice text is published on gov.uk services. Attached tender documents remain the buyer's copyright, and I could not open them to check. **Licence: uncertain.**

### Inferences
- For a product or job-template dataset: ADM-derived requirement items (gully, 1.5 kN/m² reinforcement, ≥400mm pan, lever flush, 770-850mm basin rim, 200 kg hoist ceiling) can be reused with attribution under OGL. Walsall-derived item structure and generic categories are reasonable to cite as facts. The named-product text should be re-expressed rather than copied, and the licence position flagged.
- Any item list built from these sources is illustrative, not a licensed catalogue. Brand and model names are "or equal" examples from a 2015 document.

### Gaps
- Walsall, North Norfolk and Nottinghamshire website copyright and reuse pages were not checked.
- The licence terms of the NIHE toolkit, Foundations guidance, and Camden documents could not be checked because access was blocked.
- I found the M3NHF Schedule of Rates v8 (2023, ISBN 978-1-908409-00-3, "All rights reserved"; publisher www.m3h.co.uk) as a local copy already in the shared scratchpad. It has a "Disabled Adaptations and Minor Works" section and lines such as shower-room lining boards and pattresses. I did not verify where it is hosted publicly, and its terms forbid reproduction, so no items from it are listed here.
