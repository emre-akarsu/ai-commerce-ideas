Several manufacturer PDFs, notably Geberit Duofix, are almost entirely pictorial, so text extraction gave little. Where a fact comes from a retailer page rather than the manufacturer, the row says so. Document codes and dates are given where the PDF prints them. Research date: 2026-10-06.

## Wall-hung and close-coupled WCs (Geberit Duofix, Grohe Rapid SL, Viega; Ideal Standard, Roca, Twyford)

### Takeaway
Wall-hung frames ship with the cistern, a 90 mm outlet bend, a 90 to 110 mm adaptor, M12 pan rods and fixings. The flush plate and the pan are separate purchases unless the product is a "3-in-1" set. Close-coupled suites are sold as separate pan, cistern, seat and lever codes. The pan connector is a separate configuration choice that depends on the soil outlet type.

### Cited Findings
- Geberit Duofix 111.300.00.5 scope of delivery includes an "integrated angle stop valve", "Connection set for WC, ø 90 mm; Connection bend made of PE-HD, ø 90 mm; Adaptor socket made of PE-HD, ø 90 / 110 mm; 2 threaded rods M12; 6 heavy-duty dowels M8; Sound insulation set; Fastening material". This comes from a search-tool summary attributed to the Geberit UK catalogue; the catalogue page itself returned 404 — [Geberit UK catalogue](https://catalog.geberit.co.uk/en-GB/product/PRO_100926)
- The Geberit Duofix installation manuals (966.027.00.0 (02), 12-2018; 969.031.00.0) are pictorial, with no extractable text about the scope — [Geberit 966.027.00.0](https://cdn.data.geberit.com/documents/966.027.00.0_02.pdf); [Geberit 969.031.00.0](https://cdn.data.geberit.com/documents/969.031.00.0_00.pdf)
- Grohe Rapid SL 38528001 includes "2 WC fixing bolts, front wall brackets", a GD2 6-9 l cistern, "PP outlet bend (Ø90mm), a reducer (Ø90mm/110mm), and inlet and outlet connecting sets" (search summary of retailer listings) — [B&Q](https://diy.com/departments/grohe-38528-rapid-sl-3-in-1-wc-set-1-13m-concealed-frame-cistern-plate-38772001/4005176863288_BQ.prd)
- Grohe 3-in-1 set 3886020A = "Rapid SL Element for WC - 38528001" + "Nova Cosmopolitan Chrome Flush Plate - 38765000" + "Pair Of Front Wall Brackets - 3855800M"; the wall-hung pan is not included — [QS Supplies](https://qssupplies.co.uk/bathroom-furniture-shower-taps/321819.htm)
- Armitage Shanks Contour 21 close-coupled: pan S3054 "with floor fixing kit", cistern S3654, seat S4065 and lever S4420 are separate codes. The outlet connector is an option: "S4305 Panekta outlet Connector, finned pattern to convert horizontal outlet WCs to P trap" and "S4300 … to S or turned P trap" — [Contour 21 data sheet](https://www.specifiedby.com/armitage-shanks/contour-21-close-coupled-wc-suite/armitage-shanks_Contour-21-Close-Coupled-WC-Suite_Specifications_DataSheet1.pdf)

### Inferences
- Template rule: `wall_hung_wc` requires `concealed_frame` + `flush_plate (same brand/series as the cistern: Geberit Sigma for Sigma cisterns, Grohe for Rapid SL)` + `wall_hung_pan`. A 110 mm soil connection is satisfied by the frame's 90/110 adaptor, so a separate 110 mm pan connector is not needed for these frames. That differs from the example rule in the brief. Optional items: front wall brackets (included in some Grohe sets) and a stud partition or boxing-in kit.
- Template rule: `close_coupled_wc` requires `pan` + `cistern` + `seat` + `lever (if not in the cistern)` + `pan_connector (type by outlet: P, S or offset)` + `cistern isolating valve` (see the common items below).

### Gaps
- No Viega Prevista/Eco frame, Roca or Twyford documents were retrieved within the time budget.
- I found no explicit "flush plate not supplied" wording in a Geberit text document; the conclusion is inferred from the scope list.
- I did not confirm which flush plate series are compatible with which Duofix cistern generations (Sigma 8 cm vs 12 cm, Omega).

## Basin wastes and traps (McAlpine, Viega)

### Takeaway
Basin wastes are slotted or unslotted. An unslotted waste fits basins with or without an overflow, and a slotted waste needs a basin with an overflow. UK basin traps are 32 mm with a 75 mm seal. Many basins ship without a waste.

### Cited Findings
- McAlpine unslotted pop-up all-in-one: 75 mm water seal, 1¼" compression outlet, 32 mm, 40.5 mm hole, "Suitable for basins with or without an overflow" (retailer) — [Screwfix 229CL](https://www.screwfix.com/p/mcalpine-unslotted-pop-up-all-in-one-basin-waste-with-bottle-trap-white-32mm/229cl)
- McAlpine A10 bottle trap 32 mm x 75 mm and SB10 tubular S-trap 32 mm x 75 mm — [Travis Perkins A10](https://www.travisperkins.co.uk/mcalpine-a10-seal-bottle-trap-32mm-x-75mm/p/960845); [Travis Perkins SB10](https://www.travisperkins.co.uk/mcalpine-sb10-seal-s-trap-32mm-x-75mm/p/960851)

### Inferences
- Rule: `basin` requires `basin_waste (slotted if the basin has an overflow, otherwise unslotted)` + `32mm trap (bottle or tubular, 75 mm seal)` unless an all-in-one is chosen.

### Gaps
- No McAlpine or Viega manufacturer PDF with "not supplied" wording was retrieved. I did not verify Viega basin waste data.

## Bath installation (legs, panels, waste and overflow)

### Takeaway
Ideal Standard's own guides state that panels and fixing packs are "Sold and supplied separately". Sealant, wall and floor screws and panel support battens are "not supplied". The waste and overflow are fitted "where supplied", which means they are normally a separate item.

### Cited Findings
- i.life Idealform: "Front Panel (Sold and supplied separately)", "End Panel … (Sold and supplied separately)", "Fixing Pack … (Sold and supplied separately)", "sealant (not supplied)", "3-4mm gap for sealant", a screw marked "not supplied", panel support battens "not supplied", "Fit Waste and Overflow fittings and any brassware and bath grips (where supplied)" — [Ideal Standard i.life Idealform](https://heatandplumb.com/media/ideal-standard/fitting-instructions/file-512508.pdf)
- Idealcast: "Secure wall brackets to wall (screws not provided)", "Screw bath to floor (screws not provided)", "wooden batten (not supplied)", "The baths are supplied without tap holes", panel clips "3 for front panel and 2 for end panel" — [Idealcast guide](https://images.victorianplumbing.co.uk/products/ideal-standard-white-1700-x-800mm-0th-double-ended-idealcast-bath/instructions/is_idealcast_e002301_iguide_en.pdf)
- Marmox insulated bath panel: 1800x600x30 and 800x600x30 sheets with feet; optional Marmox acrylic sealant and waterproof tape — [Marmox Bath Panel](https://www.specifiedby.com/marmox/bath-panel-kit/Marmox_Bath-Panel-Kit_Operations_Fitting-Instructions.pdf)

### Inferences
- Rule: `bath` requires `leg_set (if not supplied)` + `bath_panel (front and, for a corner install, end)` + `panel fixing pack/clips` + `waste_and_overflow` + `40mm bath trap` + `bath taps or mixer` + `sanitary silicone` + `wall/floor screws and plugs` + `timber battens`.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve a Carron or Bette document. I did not verify the bath trap size (40 mm) from a manufacturer source in this pass.

## Shower trays and wastes; shower enclosures

### Takeaway
UK stone resin trays are commonly listed with a 90 mm outlet and "shower waste … not included" (retailer wording). I did not retrieve an enclosure document.

### Cited Findings
- Stone resin trays: 90 mm outlet; shower waste and cover not included (search summary of retailer listings) — [ManoMano MX Minerals](https://www.manomano.co.uk/p/mx-minerals-quadrant-shower-tray-900mm-x-900mm-ash-grey-79610490); [Wetrooms-online Merlyn MStone](https://www.wetrooms-online.com/products/merlyn-mstone-stone-resin-shower-tray-900-x-900-x-50mm)

## Thermostatic bar and concealed valves (Mira, Grohe, Hansgrohe)

### Takeaway
Mira bar valves include offset connectors (150 mm centres) and concealing plates. Mira guides state that thread sealant and wall fixings are "(not supplied)" and that "Isolating valves must be installed".

### Cited Findings
- Mira Reflex pack contents: "1 x Mira Reflex Bar Valve", "2 x Offset Connectors", "2 x Concealing Plates", "1 x 12L/m Flow Regulator". "Apply suitable thread sealant (not supplied)". "Isolating valves must be installed close to the mixer for ease of maintenance". "For stud partitions alternative fixings may be required". Inlet centres 150 mm; max static 10 bar; min 1.0 bar with a gas water heater; 0.1 bar on a gravity system — [Mira Reflex 1196840-W2-C](https://images.victorianplumbing.co.uk/products/mira-reflex-ev-thermostatic-shower-mixer-chrome-2-1744-020/instructions/21744020.pdf)
- Mira Discovery: "using the appropriate wall fixings for the type of wall (not supplied)"; "inlet centres are 150 mm ± 24 mm"; min maintained 0.2 bar — [Mira Discovery](https://www.showerspares.com/downloads/mira-discovery-bar-mixer-1609.001.pdf)
- Mira Atom ERD: the pack includes the riser and handset kit and "2 x Wall Plugs"; "15mm pipework, 3/4" BSP to valve"; "Only use the inlet connections supplied with the product" — [Mira Atom ERD (Wickes)](https://media.wickes.co.uk/is/content/wickes/GPID_1100684411_TECH_0)

### Inferences
- Rule: `bar_mixer_shower` requires `15mm hot + cold feeds at 150 mm centres` + `2 x isolating valves` + `PTFE/thread sealant` + `wall fixings suited to the substrate`. It also needs a `riser rail kit + hose + handset` when these are not bundled (Reflex and Discovery are bar valve only; Atom ERD includes the kit). The valve must match the system pressure (combi/mains or gravity).

### Gaps
- No Grohe Grohtherm or Hansgrohe concealed valve document was retrieved. Concealed valve requirements (wall depth, box, outlet elbows) are unverified.

## Electric showers (Triton / Mira)

### Takeaway
Triton requires, but does not supply, a 45 A double pole isolating switch with 3 mm contact gap, a 30 mA RCD and an MCB sized to the kW rating. It strongly recommends at least 10 mm² cable.

### Cited Findings
- "A 45 amp double pole isolating switch with a minimum contact gap of 3mm in both poles must be incorporated in the circuit"; it "must have a mechanical indicator showing when the switch is in the OFF position"; "the wiring must be connected to the switch without the use of a plug or socket outlet" — [Triton T70Z](https://showerspares.com/downloads/triton-t70z-electric-shower-7.5kw-white-sp7007z.pdf)
- "A 30mA residual current device (RCD) MUST be installed in all UK electric and pumped shower circuits"; MCB table: 7.0 kW 30/32 A; 7.5 kW 32 A; 8.0 kW 40 A; 8.5 kW 40 A; 9.0 kW 40 A; 9.5 kW 40/45 A; 10.5 kW 45 A — [Triton T70Z](https://showerspares.com/downloads/triton-t70z-electric-shower-7.5kw-white-sp7007z.pdf)
- Cable current capacity (twin and earth, insulated wall / conduit / clipped direct): 6 mm² 32/38/46 A; 10 mm² 43/52/63 A; 16 mm² 57/69/85 A. "It is strongly recommended to use a minimum of 10mm cabling throughout the shower installation" — [Triton T70Z](https://showerspares.com/downloads/triton-t70z-electric-shower-7.5kw-white-sp7007z.pdf)
- Omnicare: "Mains pressure cold water only", min running 0.5 bar at 8 l/min, inlet 15 mm, 8.5 and 9.5 kW = 40 A MCB — [Triton Omnicare 2181515H](https://www.independent4life.co.uk/media/mageworx/downloads/attachment/file/2/5/25327%20triton%20omnicare%20installation%20and%20operating%20instructions%202181515h.pdf)

### Inferences
- Rule: `electric_shower` requires a `dedicated radial circuit` with an `MCB (rating by kW)`, `30mA RCD/RCBO`, a `45A DP pull-cord or isolating switch (3 mm gap)` and `10 mm² T&E (min, subject to an electrician's assessment)`, plus a `15mm mains cold feed` and an `isolating valve`. This is notifiable electrical work, so the template should flag it for a qualified electrician.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve a Mira electric shower guide. Only one Triton guide gives the full table (T70Z, document created 2017), so it may not be the latest issue.

## Extractor fans (Part F, timer, ducting)

### Takeaway
Vent-Axia requires a local all-pole isolator (3 mm separation), which is not part of the fan. A wall kit is optional. Part F sets the minimum extract rates and a 15-minute overrun where there is no openable window.

### Cited Findings
- "The Fan should be provided with a local isolator switch capable of disconnecting all poles, having a contact separation of at least 3mm"; "Ø100mm ducting (flexible or rigid)"; "For wall installations a Wall Kit (25 41 02 White / 25 41 00 Brown) can be used"; "A Backdraught Shutter Assembly is supplied"; condensation trap for ducted moist air; overrun "factory set to approximately 15 minutes", adjustable 1-30 min — [Vent-Axia Lo-Carbon Quadra](https://assets.cef.co.uk/downloads/pdg/vent_axia_439253_instructions/vent_axia_439253_instructions.pdf)
- ADF Vol 1 Table 1.1: kitchen (hood extracting outside) 30 l/s; kitchen otherwise 60 l/s; utility 30; bathroom 15; sanitary accommodation 6. Rooms with no openable window: fan controls continue "for at least 15 minutes after the room is vacated"; installer checks "That any run-on timers are set to at least 15 minutes" — [ADF Vol 1 2021](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/61deba42d3bf7f054fcc243d/ADF1.pdf)
- The gov.uk page notes a 2026 edition is available "for buildings subject to the 2026 standards" — [gov.uk ADF](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ventilation-approved-document-f)

### Inferences
- Rule: `bathroom_extractor` requires a `fan ≥15 l/s` + a `timer/overrun model when there is no window` + `100mm duct` + `external grille or wall/roof kit` + a `3-pole fused isolator` + a `condensation trap for vertical ducts`. Read the l/s thresholds from the deployment profile, not hard-coded (CLAUDE.md).

### Gaps
- I did not check what the 2026 ADF edition changed.
