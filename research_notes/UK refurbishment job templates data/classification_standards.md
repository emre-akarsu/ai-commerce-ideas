# Open classification systems and product-data standards for coding UK refurbishment/MRO job-template lines

Research date: 2026-10-06. Method: web search/fetch plus direct, read-only queries of the public Uniclass taxon pages (uniclass.thenbs.com/taxon/<code>) and the public bSDD REST API (api.bsdd.buildingsmart.org). Codes marked "(checked live)" were read from those sources on 2026-10-06. Items marked "(training knowledge, not verified)" come from prior knowledge and must be checked before use.

## 1. Uniclass 2015 (NBS): tables, licence, formats, relevant codes, and whether Ss acts like a kit

### Takeaway
Uniclass 2015 is the strongest candidate for the canonical code on UK template lines. It is UK-native, free, updated quarterly, licensed CC BY-ND 4.0, and it has fine-grained Pr codes for nearly every bathroom, kitchen and consumable line we need, down to "Pr_20_29_76_98 Wood screws" and "Pr_20_31_02_89 Tile adhesives". The Ss table provides system-level groupings such as "Ss_40_15_75_97 WC systems" and "Ss_25_45_88_40 Internal wall tiling systems", but these are classification headings only. Uniclass does not list which Pr products make up an Ss system, so we must build the kit/BOM content ourselves.

### Inferences
- **Coding the example lines:**
  - WC pan → Pr_40_20_93_94
  - Basin waste → Pr_65_52_25_98 (trap) or Pr_65_52_25_75, plus a local sub-type for "basin waste (slotted/unslotted, click-clack)", because Uniclass appears to lack an outlet-only code
  - Tile adhesive → Pr_20_31_02_89
  - 4.0x40 wood screw → Pr_20_29_76_98. Size, head and drive must be held as attributes; Uniclass does not encode them.
- **Template structure:** Ss codes work as template/kit headers (e.g. "Bathroom refit: WC systems Ss_40_15_75_97 + Internal wall tiling Ss_25_45_88_40"), and Pr codes work as line codes. Uniclass gives the vocabulary, not the bill of materials.
- **What CC BY-ND permits and does not permit:** it allows commercial use and verbatim redistribution with attribution. It arguably prohibits distributing a modified version of the tables, such as renamed titles, merged or added codes, or trimmed definitions published as "Uniclass". The safe pattern is:
  - store Uniclass codes and titles verbatim, with attribution and version;
  - keep our own extensions in a separate namespace (e.g. `X-Pr_20_29_76_98.wood-screw-csk-pz`) that references the Uniclass code rather than editing it.
  - Whether an internal extension that is never distributed counts as an "adapted" work is a legal question to confirm.
- The Pr table is less granular for consumables than trade catalogues. For example, "Wood screws" has no children, so it cannot distinguish countersunk, pozi or zinc variants. Those details belong in attributes (ETIM features), not codes.
- Uniclass PC (Properties and characteristics) v1.3 exists and could in principle supply attribute names. It was not examined.

### Gaps
- The exact download file format (XLSX vs CSV) and the Uniclass API terms and authentication were not verified, because the download links are rendered by JavaScript and no API docs page was fetched.
- No dedicated Uniclass code was found for a basin/sink waste outlet (plug and waste), a WC pan fixing kit, or flexible tap connectors. The free-text search on uniclass.thenbs.com is JavaScript-rendered and could not be queried.
- The Ss_40_15_75 page showed two titles for some codes (e.g. _92 as both "Urinal systems" and "Washbasin systems"; _94, _96, _98 likewise). This is likely an artefact of the page listing current and superseded entries. The current title must be confirmed from the downloaded table before use.
- No published NBS mapping from Ss to Pr (a kit/BOM) was found. Whether NBS Chorus specification clauses provide such linkages is out of scope here and is a paid product.

## 2. RICS NRM 1/2/3: structure, cost and licence, mapping to BoQ items

### Takeaway
NRM2 gives the UK bill of quantities a 41-section work-section structure. Section 32 "Furniture, fittings and equipment" (where sanitaryware is commonly measured) and section 33 "Drainage above ground" are the relevant ones. However, NRM is RICS copyright with an all-rights-reserved notice, not an open licence. It is suitable as a grouping and mapping label (section number and title), not as a dataset to embed or redistribute.

### Cited Findings
- NRM2 2nd edition UK was published October 2021, effective 1 December 2021. A PDF is hosted at rics.org (https://www.rics.org/content/dam/ricsglobal/documents/standards/october_2021_nrm_2.pdf). It is a free download for RICS members or can be purchased from RICS Books. Reported copyright wording: "No part of the work may be reproduced or used in any form or by any means without the written permission of RICS or in line with the rules of an existing licence." — [RICS NRM2 PDF (search result)](https://www.rics.org/content/dam/ricsglobal/documents/standards/october_2021_nrm_2.pdf), [Designing Buildings NRM2](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/NRM2:_Detailed_measurement_for_building_works)
- **NRM2 work sections (41)**, including: 4 Alterations, repairs and conservation; 16 Carpentry; 20 Proprietary linings and partitions; 22 General joinery; 28 Floor, wall, ceiling and roof finishings; 29 Decoration; 32 Furniture, fittings and equipment; 33 Drainage above ground; 38 Mechanical services; 39 Electrical services; 41 Builder's work in connection with services. — [Designing Buildings NRM2](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/NRM2:_Detailed_measurement_for_building_works)
- NRM2 was first published April 2012 and became operative on 1 January 2013. All three NRM volumes were republished in new editions between October 2021 and October 2022. — [Designing Buildings NRM2](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/NRM2:_Detailed_measurement_for_building_works)

### Inferences
- **For template lines:** store an `nrm2_section` field (e.g. "32" for WC pan, basin and taps; "33" for waste pipework; "28" for tiling, tile adhesive and grout; "20" for plasterboard and studs) as a secondary grouping. This helps quantity surveyors and contractors read a template as a BoQ.
- Section numbers and titles are short factual labels, and using them as references is low-risk. Reproducing NRM2 measurement rules, coverage rules or item descriptions in our product would need RICS permission.
- NRM1 (cost planning, group elements such as "Services > Sanitary installations") and NRM3 (maintenance/renewal cost planning) are more relevant to MRO lifecycle work. Their element numbering was not verified here.

### Gaps
- The exact NRM1 element codes (e.g. "5.1 Sanitary installations") and the NRM3 structure were not verified from a primary source.
- Whether rics.org now offers NRM2 free to non-members (the PDF URL is public) or only to members was not confirmed. Either way, the copyright notice restricts reuse.
- It was not verified which NRM2 section sanitary appliances are measured in. Practice commonly places them in section 32 or 38. Confirm against the document.

## 3. ETIM (International/UK), ECLASS, UNSPSC, GS1 GPC: licences, formats, relevant classes, attributes

### Takeaway
ETIM is the best open source of per-class **attributes** (features) for spec-level RFQ lines. It is ODC-By licensed, free, and reachable via bSDD. It has classes for wood screws, tile adhesive, siphons/traps, washbasins, toilet pans, steel studs and gypsum board. ECLASS is paid (from about €700 per release) and should be avoided as a canonical code. UNSPSC is free to browse and download (PDF) but is UNDP-copyrighted with restrictive terms, and it is coarse. GS1 GPC is free and retail-oriented, useful only as an optional mapping for merchant/GTIN data.

### Cited Findings

Attribution: "You must attribute any public use of the database, or works produced from the database, in the manner specified in the licence... keep intact any notices on the original database." — [ETIM licence info](https://www.etim-international.com/?p=1811)
- ETIM xChange (the product-data exchange format) is Apache License 2.0. — [ETIM licence info](https://www.etim-international.com/?p=1811)
- Translations are maintained by national ETIM organisations, which may restrict access. English (master) is open to all. — [ETIM licence info](https://www.etim-international.com/?p=1811)
- Access: ETIM Viewer, Classification Management Tool, ETIM API (etimapi.etim-international.com), and model release downloads. — [ETIM licence info](https://www.etim-international.com/?p=1811)
- The ETIM Viewer offers an "English (UK)" language option alongside "ETIM English" and "English (US)". — [ETIM Viewer EC011289](https://viewer.etim-international.com/class/EC011289)

### Inferences
- **ETIM is the right source of "spec" attributes for RFQ lines.** Example: a 4.0x40 wood screw = ETIM EC002385 with EF004757=4.0 mm, EF001438=40 mm, EF000012=countersunk, EF000013 or EF018180=PZ2, EF000139=zinc plated. This gives a structured, supplier-neutral spec that a merchant can quote against, which fits hard rule 3 (attributes with provenance).
- ODC-By allows adaptation and redistribution with attribution. ETIM can therefore be embedded and extended (unlike Uniclass ND). This makes ETIM suitable for the attribute schema even though Uniclass is the UK-native line code.
- ETIM is strong for installer/wholesale goods (plumbing, electrical, fixings) and thinner for wet trades and finishes (tiles, plaster). The tile adhesive class exists, but coverage of ceramic tiles, grouts and plasterboard accessories should be checked class by class.
- ECLASS's per-company paid licence, plus the unknown terms on redistributing class data inside a SaaS product, make it unsuitable as canonical. At most it is an optional mapping if a customer already holds a licence.
- UNSPSC is too coarse for template lines and its terms are restrictive or unclear. Treat it as an optional mapping for customers whose ERP/procurement requires it, and do not ship the codeset.
- GPC is designed for retail GTIN data pools. It is useful only to join to merchant/GTIN feeds, not to describe trade spec lines.

### Gaps
- ETIM UK, the national body, and its membership terms for the English (UK) translation were not researched. It is unclear whether the UK translation is open or members-only. The ETIM licence page lists English master as open but did not explicitly list English (UK).
- No ETIM classes were found for ceramic/porcelain tiles, tile grout, silicone sanitary sealant as such, or a basin waste outlet (as opposed to siphon/trap). These need a targeted lookup in the ETIM Viewer, which is JavaScript-rendered and could not be searched by script.
- ETIM API authentication and rate terms were not checked. bSDD read access worked without authentication.
- UNSPSC class codes for sanitaryware and fixings were not verified. Examples such as Segment 30 "Structures and Building and Construction Components" and Segment 31 fasteners are training knowledge, not verified.
- No first-hand quote of the current UNSPSC Terms of Use or the GS1 GPC licence could be obtained (403 or unrelated content).
- The ECLASS release current in 2026 and its terms on redistributing class data were not confirmed.

## 4. buildingSMART IFC, bSDD and COBie: entity types, licence, API

### Takeaway
IFC gives coarse object types (IfcSanitaryTerminal with predefined types such as WASHHANDBASIN and TOILETPAN) that are useful for BIM interoperability but too coarse for procurement lines. bSDD is a free, unauthenticated-for-read REST API that already hosts Uniclass 2015 (CC BY-ND), ETIM 10.1 (ODC-By) and IFC 4.3 (CC BY-ND). It can serve as a single lookup, URI and cross-mapping service for template line codes.

### Cited Findings
- bSDD dictionaries (checked live): "IFC 4.3" (owner buildingSMART International, licence CC BY-ND 4.0); "ETIM 10.1" (ODC-By); "Uniclass 2015" version 1 (NBS, CC BY-ND 4.0); "CCI Construction" 1.0 (Molio, MIT); "GS1 Identifiers" (GS1 IP Policy). Each dictionary has a URI, e.g. `https://identifier.buildingsmart.org/uri/etim/etim/10.1/class/EC002385`. — [bSDD API Dictionary list](https://api.bsdd.buildingsmart.org/api/Dictionary/v1)
- The IFC 4.3 bSDD class IfcSanitaryTerminal is defined as "a fixed appliance or terminal usually supplied with water and used for drinking, cleaning or foul water disposal or that is an item of equipment directly used with such an appliance or terminal". — [bSDD Class API IfcSanitaryTerminal](https://api.bsdd.buildingsmart.org/api/Class/v1?Uri=https://identifier.buildingsmart.org/uri/buildingsmart/ifc/4.3/class/IfcSanitaryTerminal)
- bSDD service terms (via search snippets): "The service is provided by buildingSMART for free". Publishing in bSDD grants buildingSMART International rights to redistribute content via the search page and API. Each dictionary owner chooses a licence and must provide a "LicenseUrl". — [buildingSMART bSDD](https://www.buildingsmart.org/users/services/buildingsmart-data-dictionary/), [bSDD licence page (403 on direct fetch)](https://technical.buildingsmart.org/?p=4831)
- Read access to `api.bsdd.buildingsmart.org` endpoints (Dictionary/v1, TextSearch/v2, Class/v1 with IncludeClassProperties) worked without an API key on 2026-10-06 (observed directly).

### Inferences
- **IFC mapping (training knowledge, not verified against IFC 4.3 docs):**
  - IfcSanitaryTerminal (a subtype of IfcFlowTerminal) has PredefinedType values BATH, BIDET, CISTERN, SANITARYFOUNTAIN, SHOWER, SINK, TOILETPAN, URINAL, WASHHANDBASIN, WCSEAT, USERDEFINED, NOTDEFINED.
  - IfcWasteTerminal covers traps and wastes (e.g. WASTETRAP, FLOORTRAP).
  - IfcMechanicalFastener (e.g. SCREW) and IfcFastener (e.g. GLUE) cover fixings and adhesives.
  - IfcCovering (CLADDING/FLOORING) covers tiles, and IfcValve covers taps and isolators.
  - Example mappings: WC pan → IfcSanitaryTerminal.TOILETPAN; basin waste → IfcWasteTerminal.WASTETRAP; tile adhesive → IfcFastener.GLUE; wood screw → IfcMechanicalFastener.SCREW.
- Use bSDD URIs as the stored identifiers for the Uniclass and ETIM codes. This gives stable, resolvable IDs, and bSDD relations may provide some Uniclass↔IFC↔ETIM mappings for free. Whether such relations exist for these classes was not checked.
- COBie (UK: BS 1192-4:2014, a spreadsheet handover schema; training knowledge, not verified) is a handover/FM format, not a classification. It is relevant only if the product exports asset registers for MRO after a refurbishment. Its Type/Component sheets carry a classification column where the Uniclass Pr code would go.

### Gaps
- The IFC 4.3 documentation page for IfcSanitaryTerminalTypeEnum could not be fetched (redirect, then content not rendered), so the enumeration values above are unverified.
- bSDD's own licence/terms page returned 403. Its terms are reported only via search snippets.

## 5. NBS National BIM Library / NBS Source and BIMobject: downloadable data and terms

### Takeaway
Both are manufacturer-content platforms whose terms permit downloading objects for use in your own projects. BIMobject explicitly forbids redistribution, scraping, building competing services, and AI training. Neither should be used as a source for a redistributable product list. The NBS Source terms were not retrieved.

## 6. Recommendation: canonical code vs secondary mappings (grounded in licence terms)

### Takeaway
Use **Uniclass 2015 Pr** as the canonical line code and **Uniclass Ss** as the template/kit header code. Both are UK-native, free, and licensed CC BY-ND 4.0 for commercial use with attribution, stored verbatim. Use **ETIM (ODC-By)** as the attribute and feature schema for spec-level RFQ lines, plus a secondary class code. Keep **NRM2 section**, **IFC type**, and optionally **UNSPSC/GPC** as secondary mapping fields only. Do not embed ECLASS, BIMobject or NBS Source content.

### Cited Findings
- Uniclass: CC BY-ND 4.0, free, commercial use allowed, quarterly releases. — [Uniclass download](https://uniclass.thenbs.com/download)
- ETIM: ODC-By 1.0, which allows share, create and adapt with attribution, and has per-class features (e.g. 39 properties on EC002385). — [ETIM licence](https://www.etim-international.com/?p=1811), [bSDD Class API EC002385](https://api.bsdd.buildingsmart.org/api/Class/v1?Uri=https://identifier.buildingsmart.org/uri/etim/etim/10.1/class/EC002385&IncludeClassProperties=true)
- ECLASS: paid per company. — [ECLASS prices](https://eclass.eu/en/eclass-standard/prices)
- UNSPSC: UNDP copyright, "All other uses are prohibited". — [UNSPSC FAQs (snippet)](https://www.unspsc.org/faqs)
- NRM2: RICS all-rights-reserved notice. — [RICS NRM2](https://www.rics.org/content/dam/ricsglobal/documents/standards/october_2021_nrm_2.pdf)
- BIMobject: no redistribution, no AI training. — [BIMobject terms](https://business.bimobject.com/terms-of-service-eula)

### Inferences
- **Proposed template-line schema:**
  - `uniclass_pr` (canonical, verbatim code, title and version, e.g. "Pr_20_29_76_98 Wood screws", v1.43)
  - `uniclass_ss` (on the template/kit header)
  - `etim_class` (e.g. EC002385) and `etim_features` {EF004757: 4.0 mm, EF001438: 40 mm, ...}, with ETIM version
  - `nrm2_section` (e.g. 32 or 33)
  - `ifc_type` (e.g. IfcSanitaryTerminal.TOILETPAN)
  - optional `unspsc` and `gpc_brick`
  - each mapping should carry `source`/`confidence`, in line with hard rule 3
- **Worked examples:**
  - WC pan: Pr_40_20_93_94 | ETIM EC011289 (features: outlet location, rimless, wall-hung fixing centres, dimensions) | NRM2 32 (verify) | IfcSanitaryTerminal.TOILETPAN
  - Basin waste: Pr_65_52_25_98 Waste traps (no outlet-only code found) | ETIM EC010195 P-, S-, bottle siphon, or the waste-fitting class (to verify) | NRM2 33 | IfcWasteTerminal.WASTETRAP
  - Tile adhesive: Pr_20_31_02_89 | ETIM EC011707 | NRM2 28 | IfcFastener.GLUE (unverified)
  - 4.0x40 wood screw: Pr_20_29_76_98 | ETIM EC002385, EF004757=4.0, EF001438=40 | NRM2: not separately measured (fixings are usually deemed included) | IfcMechanicalFastener.SCREW
- **Licence hygiene:**
  - Ship Uniclass titles unaltered, with attribution text "Uniclass 2015 © NBS, CC BY-ND 4.0, vX.XX". Put our sub-types in a separate namespace instead of editing Uniclass.
  - ETIM attribution should state the ETIM version and the ODC-By licence.
  - Template content we author (quantities, kit compositions) is our own work, and must be labelled synthetic/illustrative per CLAUDE.md until validated.
- **Profile configurability (ADR-011):** classification system choice and versions are market-specific (Uniclass is UK). They belong in the deployment profile, e.g. `classification.canonical: uniclass2015`, rather than hard-coded.

### Gaps
- A legal reading is needed of whether storing Uniclass codes alongside our own extended sub-codes and attribute sets counts as a "derivative" under CC BY-ND when served to customers.
- No published, licensed Uniclass↔ETIM crosswalk was confirmed. The bSDD relations should be checked before building one by hand.
