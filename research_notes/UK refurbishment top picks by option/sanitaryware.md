# UK Bathroom Sanitaryware: Budget / Most-Used / Premium Options (2024-2026)

Research date: 2026-10-06. All prices are **observed retail prices** (inc. VAT unless stated) from retailer pages or search snippets on that date, or from the dated guide cited. Several Screwfix prices were promotional ("save x%") and will move. Brands are examples only. No paid market report (AMA Research, Mintel, BMA) was accessible, so this note has **no measured share data** for WC types, bath materials or tray sizes. "Most used" calls rest on retailer statements, catalogue counts and social-housing specs. Treat them as indicative.

## What share of UK WCs sold are close-coupled vs wall-hung?

### Takeaway
I found no published unit-share figure. The qualitative evidence agrees that close-coupled is the UK default and wall-hung is a growing premium or new-build option. Pre-select: **close-coupled, standard height, rimless, soft-close seat**.

### Cited Findings
- Victorian Plumbing says close-coupled toilets are supplied "more than any other type of toilet" and are "the go-to choice in most UK homes" (page viewed Oct 2026, no publication date) — [Victorian Plumbing close-coupled buying guide](https://www.victorianplumbing.co.uk/buying-guides/toilets/close-coupled-toilets)
- On that retailer's close-coupled category (Oct 2026), 253 of the listed products have soft-close seats against 48 with standard seats. 132 of about 310 are rimless. 37 are comfort height. The largest price clusters are £150-£200 (62 products) and £200-£300 (111 products) — [Victorian Plumbing](https://www.victorianplumbing.co.uk/buying-guides/toilets/close-coupled-toilets)
- Victorian Plumbing support: "most toilets in British homes these days are close-coupled" — [VP support article](https://support.victorianplumbing.co.uk/hc/en-us/articles/4410978478609-What-are-close-coupled-toilets)
- Wall-hung demand is shown only by a survey that a manufacturer paid for (Geberit, 5,000+ UK homeowners, published 2 Sep 2025). 73% said a wall-hung toilet fitted as standard in a new home would appeal. 56% prefer wall-hung in new builds. 44% would choose wall-hung over a traditional toilet. This measures stated preference, not sales — [KBB Focus](https://kbbfocus.com/news/5341-new-research-from-geberit-highlights-trend-for-wall-hung-wcs)
- AMA Research expected "minimalist, wall-hung and counter-top designs" to affect sanitaryware volume demand. It also gives baths and sanitaryware a 35% share of the bathroom market by value. No split by WC type was visible — [KBB Review summary of AMA Research](https://www.kbbreview.com/1167/news/bathroom-market-sees-steady-improvement/); a full AMA "Bathroom Market Report – UK 2024-2028" exists but is paywalled — [Barbour Product Search listing](https://www.barbourproductsearch.info/bathroom-market-report-uk-2024-2028-prod1300415.html)
- Screwfix close-coupled, rimless, soft-close prices observed Oct 2026:

## Standard bath size and material most fitted in UK homes and social housing

### Takeaway
1700x700 mm is the UK standard size. Acrylic is the most sold material in retail. Enamelled steel shows up in social-housing specifications (e.g. Camden). Pre-select: **1700x700 single-ended acrylic straight bath**.

### Cited Findings
- "The current standard bath size in the UK is 1700mm in length and 700mm wide". It is described as the metric replacement for the imperial 5'6" bath — [Sanctuary Bathrooms](https://www.sanctuary-bathrooms.co.uk/blog/understanding-bath-sizes/); [Bathroom City guide](https://www.bathroomcity.co.uk/blog/standard-bath-size-guide)
- Northern Ireland housing association design standards give a 1700x700 bath for single-storey dwellings — [DfC NI HAGDS bathroom standards (PDF)](https://www.communities-ni.gov.uk/sites/default/files/publications/communities/hagds-tba-standards-bathroom-appliances-and-space-requirements.pdf)
- A Camden Council social housing specification lists a **steel single-ended bath 1700x700** and a **550x440 mm one-tap-hole basin**. This comes from the search snippet. The PDF returned HTTP 503 when I tried to fetch it, so it is unverified in full — [Camden doc](https://camdocs.camden.gov.uk/CMWebDrawer/Record/10566578/file/document)
- Acrylic is described as the most popular bath material, "UK retailers ... sell more acrylic baths than steel baths". Acrylic is usually cheaper than steel of comparable quality. Steel is enamelled, more impact- and scratch-resistant, and can be re-enamelled — [Drench](https://www.drench.co.uk/blog/buying-guides/acrylic-or-steel-baths); [Sanctuary steel vs acrylic](https://www.sanctuary-bathrooms.co.uk/guides/steel-or-acrylic-bath); [Bathroom Mountain](https://www.bathroommountain.co.uk/inspiration-and-advice/steel-vs-acrylic-baths)
- Budget DIY bundles default to acrylic 1700x700. The Plumbsure Bodmin suite (B&Q group) pairs it with a close-coupled WC and a pedestal basin — [B&Q/DIY.ie Plumbsure Bodmin](https://www.diy.ie/departments/plumbsure-bodmin-close-coupled-toilet-with-standard-close-seat/3663602205760_BQ.prd)
- Ideal Standard Tesi 1700x700 is listed in NBS Source in "Idealform Plus", a reinforced acrylic. It is an example of a specifier-grade acrylic bath — [NBS Source](https://source.thenbs.com/product/tesi-170-cm-x-70-cm-idealform-plus-bath/mgwZrxEnrVftv4uJ1c5a6f)

### Inferences (proposed option tags for bath)
| Tag | Option | One-line spec |
|---|---|---|
| budget | 1700x700 single-ended acrylic, standard thickness (e.g. 5 mm) | Straight bath, legs, cradle or baseboard |
| **most_used (default)** | 1700x700 single-ended acrylic, reinforced | Straight bath, end taps or mixer at the tap end |
| social-housing variant | 1700x700 enamelled steel single-ended | Specified for durability (Camden) |
| premium | 1700x750/800 double-ended acrylic, or freestanding / cast-iron | Centre waste with taps on the side wall, or roll-top |

- Single-ended is the default because suite bundles from VP, B&Q and Wickes specify "single-ended 1700mm" ([bestbuilders comparison, Jul 2026](https://www.bestbuilders.co.uk/guides/compare/compare-bathroom-suites-best-value-options-in-2026-uk); [Plumbsure Bodmin](https://www.diy.ie/departments/plumbsure-bodmin-close-coupled-toilet-with-standard-close-seat/3663602205760_BQ.prd)).

### Gaps

## Most common shower tray size and material

### Takeaway
Stone resin, low-profile (about 40-45 mm), is now the mainstream material. 1200x800 rectangular trays and 800/900 square or quadrant trays are the most widely stocked sizes. I found no data ranking tray sizes by sales. Pre-select: **stone resin, low-profile, 1200x800 rectangular (walk-in or sliding-door)**, with 900x900 quadrant as the alternative for small rooms.

### Cited Findings
- Common UK tray sizes listed are 700x700, 800x800, 900x900, 1200x800 and 1400x900 (the larger rectangles for walk-ins). Stone resin is called "the leading material".
- Cost ranges in that guide (undated, retrieved Oct 2026):
  - stone resin about £180-£440
  - acrylic about £120-£180
  - low-profile £160-£400
- Source for the sizes, material and costs above — [Bathroom Mountain shower tray guide](https://www.bathroommountain.co.uk/inspiration-and-advice/best-shower-trays/)
- Many retailers stock 1200x800x45 mm stone resin trays, rectangular and quadrant (e.g. Pearl) — [Appliances Direct Pearl 1200x800](https://www.appliancesdirect.co.uk/p/pea120x80/pearl-pea120x80-shower-tray); [Toolstation Nuie Hydro45](https://www.toolstation.com/nuie-hydro45-anti-slip-rectangular-shower-tray/pAL500)

### Inferences (proposed option tags for shower tray)
| Tag | Option | One-line spec | Price band (Bathroom Mountain, retrieved Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| budget | Acrylic-capped stone resin or ABS/acrylic tray, 800x800 / 900x900 | Light, quick to fit | about £120-£180 |
| **most_used (default)** | Stone resin, 45 mm low profile, 1200x800 rectangle (or 900x900 quadrant) | Dense, rigid, with 90 mm waste | about £180-£300 (inferred within the £180-£440 range) |
| premium | Slate-effect or anti-slip stone resin, 1400x900 or larger, or a flush wet-room former | Textured, coloured or level access | up to about £440+ |

### Gaps

- Prices come from one retailer guide. Individual SKU prices were not captured.

## Enclosure / screen

### Cited Findings
- "Typical shower glass thickness is 6mm". The cheapest enclosures use 4 mm glass, mid-price 6 mm, and premium 8 mm. Sanctuary recommends 8 mm for enclosures, 6-8 mm for over-bath screens, and 8-10 mm for walk-in/wet-room panels — [Sanctuary Bathrooms](https://www.sanctuary-bathrooms.co.uk/blog/shower-glass-thickness-6mm-8mm-10mm); [Sanctuary glass-thickness guide](https://www.sanctuary-bathrooms.co.uk/guides/shower-glass-thickness-should-i-get-6mm-8mm-or-10mm)
- Observed 6 mm enclosure prices:
  - Victorian Plumbing 6 mm range about £339.95-£529.95 (search snippet, Oct 2026) — [VP 6mm enclosures](https://victorianplumbing.co.uk/shower-enclosures/ranges/6mm-glass-shower-enclosures)
  - Merlyn 6 mm about £377-£1,367 (RRP, search snippet) — [Merlyn 6mm](https://www.merlynshowering.com/products/find-your-perfect-shower/shower-enclosures-by-style/space-saving-practical-shower-enclosures/merlyn-6mm-glass-shower-enclosures.html)
- Suite bundles typically include a bath/shower mixer with a bath screen, which supports the bath screen as the default "shower over bath" setup in UK suites — [bestbuilders comparison (aggregator, Jul 2026)](https://www.bestbuilders.co.uk/guides/compare/compare-bathroom-suites-best-value-options-in-2026-uk)

## Basin

### Cited Findings
- "A 500mm or 600mm basin is the most commonly fitted size in a family bathroom". Full pedestal basins are described as "one of the most popular basins sold in the UK" — [Bathroom City pedestal basin guide](https://www.bathroomcity.co.uk/blog/pedestal-basin-buying-guide)
- Vanity widths: compact 400-550 mm (cloakroom), standard 600-750 mm, large 800-900 mm, double 1000 mm+ — [Bathroom Mountain vanity sizes](https://www.bathroommountain.co.uk/inspiration-and-advice/vanity-unit-sizes/)
- Social-housing example: a 550x440 one-tap-hole basin (Camden spec; snippet only) — [Camden doc](https://camdocs.camden.gov.uk/CMWebDrawer/Record/10566578/file/document)
- Budget bundles pair a 550 mm basin with either a flat-pack vanity or a full pedestal — [ManoMano Ambon suite](https://www.manomano.co.uk/p/ambon-1700mm-bath-550mm-basin-flat-pack-vanity-unit-close-coupled-toilet-shower-slider-raill-kit-bath-and-mono-mixer-wastes-white-49495883); [Plumbsure Bodmin](https://www.diy.ie/departments/plumbsure-bodmin-close-coupled-toilet-with-standard-close-seat/3663602205760_BQ.prd)

### Inferences
| Tag | Option |
|---|---|
| budget | 500-550 mm one-tap-hole basin on a full pedestal |
| **most_used (default)** | 550 mm full-pedestal basin (600 mm where space allows) |
| alt | 500 mm semi-pedestal or wall-hung (e.g. Wickes Siena 500 mm) |
| premium | 600-800 mm vanity unit with basin (floor-standing or wall-hung) |
- Pedestal basins remain the bundle default, but the vanity unit is a common upgrade. I found no data ranking the two by sales.

## Bathroom suites as a bundle

### Cited Findings
- A typical 4-piece suite is a close-coupled WC, a basin with pedestal, a single-ended 1700 mm bath, and a bath/shower mixer with screen.
- Price bands (benchmarked May 2026, page updated Jul 2026, installation excluded):
  - budget about £300-£550
  - mid about £550-£1,100
  - premium about £900-£2,400+
- Example ranges from the same comparison:
  - Victoria Plum Atlanta £300-£550
  - B&Q Cooke & Lewis £450-£950
  - Wickes £550-£1,100
  - Drench £900-£2,400
- This source is a third-party aggregator, so verify before relying on it. Source for all of the above — [bestbuilders.co.uk comparison](https://www.bestbuilders.co.uk/guides/compare/compare-bathroom-suites-best-value-options-in-2026-uk)
- Example packaged prices (snippet, Oct 2026): Victorian Plumbing Oxford 1700 mm traditional suite £429.95 and Keswick 4-piece £399.95 — [VP Oxford](https://www.victorianplumbing.co.uk/oxford-complete-traditional-bathroom-package); [VP Keswick](https://www.victorianplumbing.co.uk/keswick-4-piece-traditional-bathroom-suite)

### Inferences
- Default suite to pre-select: close-coupled rimless soft-close WC, 550 mm full-pedestal basin, 1700x700 single-ended acrylic bath, 6 mm bath screen. Mid band is about £550-£1,100 (May-Jul 2026 benchmark). Budget is about £300-£550, and premium about £900-£2,400+ (wall-hung WC, vanity, 8 mm glass, double-ended or freestanding bath).
