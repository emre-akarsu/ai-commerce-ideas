## Which plasterboard type and size do UK trades use by default (bathroom stud partition vs ceiling vs wet zone)?

### Takeaway
The UK default is **12.5 mm tapered-edge standard board, 2400x1200**, used for partitions and ceilings. Typical examples are Gyproc WallBoard and Knauf/Siniat equivalents. In bathrooms the step-up is **12.5 mm moisture-resistant (MR, green) board** for walls and ceiling outside the direct splash zone. Inside a shower enclosure or wet room, gypsum board (MR included) is no longer an acceptable tile substrate on NHBC-warranted new homes. There a cement or XPS backer board plus tanking is expected.

## Timber vs metal studs in UK domestic refurb: which is the default and why?

### Takeaway
**Timber CLS (38x63 mm for light partitions, 38x89 mm for stiffer, taller or sound-insulated walls) is the UK domestic refurb default.** Metal C-stud (about 50 or 70 mm, e.g. Gypframe 70 S 50) is the commercial default and a growing premium or alternative in housing. Metal is straighter, faster to erect and non-combustible, but needs different skills and fixings for heavy items.

## Which backer board is most used behind tiles in UK showers?

### Takeaway
There is no single source with a hard ranking. Evidence points to **XPS cement-coated boards (Marmox Multiboard, Jackoboard, Wedi; Marmox most visible in UK trade press and tiler forums) and fibre-cement boards (Hardiebacker, NoMorePly)** as the two mainstream shower choices. MR plasterboard plus tanking survives as the budget refurb route but is now excluded by NHBC for wet rooms and power-shower bathrooms. XPS boards are favoured by tilers for low weight and easy cutting. Cement boards are favoured for rigidity and heavy tiles.

## Standard screw lengths and spacings tied to those boards; standard fixings

### Takeaway
For a single 12.5 mm board, **drywall screws of 25 mm (metal stud) or 32-38 mm (timber)** are standard. Screwfix's best-selling plasterboard screws are 3.5 mm bugle-head black-phosphate drywall screws in 25, 32 and 38 mm. NHBC baseline spacing is **300 mm centres on walls and 230 mm on ceilings**. BG guidance is about 230 mm in the field and 150 mm at perimeters. Tighter spacing is common practice. For general fixing, the UK default wall plug is a universal nylon plug such as Fischer DuoPower or Rawlplug Uno. For plasterboard, metal cavity or large universal fixings (e.g. Bullfix) are standard.
